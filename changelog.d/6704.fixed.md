**The cert-expiry context check reads an attribute whose `#`, `!` and `[` are separated by whitespace or a newline (#6704).** `# [cfg(any())]`, `#` and `[cfg(any())]` on two lines, and `# ! [cfg(any())]` are valid Rust and compile the item out, but the #6560 scan matched only the joined spelling, so the guarded line stayed GREEN. The scan now works on Rust tokens and joins the three tokens across any trivia; `(wrap-attr-spaced)`, `(wrap-attr-newline)` and `(wrap-inner-spaced)` pin it.
