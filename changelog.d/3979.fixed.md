- **[data-integrity] A webhook delivery still queued at the shutdown drain
  deadline vanished without a trace, and the shutdown log said it had not**
  ([#3979](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3979)).
  A delivery's `subscription_events` audit row is written by its worker,
  and the worker runs only once the delivery holds a dispatch permit
  (`AI_MEMORY_WEBHOOK_DISPATCH_CONCURRENCY`, default 32) and the blocking
  pool has started it. When the 30 s shutdown drain expired, a delivery
  still waiting for either was dropped with the runtime. It had no audit
  row and no `subscription_dlq` row, so `memory_subscription_replay` and
  `memory_subscription_dlq_list` could not see it. Meanwhile the CLI's
  shutdown WARN, a `daemon_runtime.rs` comment, `drain_dispatches`'s doc,
  `write_events.rs` and `docs/API_REFERENCE.md` all said every admitted
  delivery had a persisted audit row. The memory write itself was never
  at risk; the notification record and its at-least-once delivery were.
  This takes more than 32 deliveries in flight, since a failing receiver
  holds its permit for its ~26 s retry ladder.
  - **Fix.** Every admitted delivery is registered in memory until its
    worker claims it. When the drain misses its deadline, it records every
    unclaimed delivery to `subscription_dlq` with the full payload, zero
    attempts and `last_error = "shutdown_unstarted"`. Removal from the
    registry is the single ownership hand-off, done under its lock. A
    delivery is therefore recorded by exactly one of the worker and the
    drain, and a recorded delivery is never sent late. If the DLQ write
    fails (for example at the per-subscription DLQ cap), the delivery is
    logged at ERROR with its identity and stays registered, so a runtime
    that outlives the deadline can still deliver it. This covers both the
    sqlite and postgres daemons: both write and read the DLQ through the
    same sqlite audit mirror.
  - **Shutdown log.** Both the CLI and the daemon now log the count of
    deliveries that had started (and have an audit row), were recorded to
    the DLQ, and could not be recorded (`DispatchDrainReport`,
    `drain_dispatches_with_report`). The five false texts are corrected.
  - **Not fixed: a crash.** SIGKILL, an OOM kill or an abort skips the
    drain. A delivery that had not started is then lost, because until its
    worker runs it exists only in memory. The docs now say so. Closing that
    needs the audit row persisted at admission ([#3980](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3980)).
  - **Tests.** `tests/webhook_shutdown_unstarted_dlq_3979.rs` pins one
    permit, queues a second delivery behind a receiver that never ACKs in
    time, drains with a short timeout and drops the runtime. It asserts
    each delivery has an audit row or a `shutdown_unstarted` DLQ row, and
    never both. It fails on the pre-fix code. Unit tests in
    `src/subscriptions/unstarted.rs` pin the hand-off and the fail-closed
    keep-on-DLQ-failure path.
