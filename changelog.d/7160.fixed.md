**Self-hosted runner `target/debug` is now size-capped (#7160).** The persistent
`target/` of the self-hosted CI jobs grew without bound (166G on each f2 runner,
root filesystem down to 4.7G free). New `scripts/ci-sweep-target.py` deletes the
oldest-mtime entries of `target/debug/{deps,incremental,build,.fingerprint}`
until the tree is under `--max-bytes`, refuses any target outside
`$GITHUB_WORKSPACE` (exit 2) unless `--allow-root`, and never follows symlinks
(the target is opened by one `O_NOFOLLOW` descriptor walk from `/` and every
scan and delete is relative to those handles, so a swap of any component
before the open or after the scan is refused, not followed; `--allow-root`
needs a canonical target; the parents of the target are opened with `O_PATH` where
the platform has it, so a search-only parent still sweeps on Linux, while on macOS
every parent must be readable by the sweeping account).
`ci.yml` (every self-hosted `check` leg: linux-fed and both macos-fed legs,
skipped on docs-only changes; the linux-fed `shared_target` leg sweeps its own per-runner `$CARGO_TARGET_DIR` with `--allow-root`
because #7188 moved its build outside the workspace), `cert-postgres-age.yml` and
`postgres-ignored.yml` run it as a final `always()` self-hosted-only step with
a 40 GiB `SELF_HOSTED_TARGET_CAP_BYTES`. A symlinked `target/debug` or swept
directory, or a target path component that is not a directory
(`refused: <path> is not a directory`), is refused (exit 2), `--max-bytes` must be positive (exit 3), a
missing, unknown or unparsable argument exits 5 (#7167), and an unreadable
directory or entry at any depth warns on stderr and is skipped (exit 0), and a top-level
entry deeper than 300 levels is skipped whole with one warning naming it (none of
it is deleted, never a partial delete). An entry that holds an unreadable or read-only
directory is likewise kept whole (checked before any delete) and the summary line reports
`removed=` entries and `freed=` bytes really deleted. When
nothing at all could be sized because every swept directory or entry was
unreadable the script exits 4, which turns the always-run step red. Tests: `scripts/test/test_ci_sweep_target.py` and `scripts/test/test_workflow_sweep_7160.py`,
both run by the `classify` job of `ci.yml` (ubuntu-latest, where the `O_PATH` test executes).
