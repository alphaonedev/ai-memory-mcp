**Self-hosted runner `target/debug` is now size-capped (#7160).** The persistent
`target/` of the self-hosted CI jobs grew without bound (166G on each f2 runner,
root filesystem down to 4.7G free). New `scripts/ci-sweep-target.py` deletes the
oldest-mtime entries of `target/debug/{deps,incremental,build,.fingerprint}`
until the tree is under `--max-bytes`, refuses any target outside
`$GITHUB_WORKSPACE` (exit 2) unless `--allow-root`, and never follows symlinks.
`ci.yml` (every self-hosted `check` leg: linux-fed and both macos-fed legs,
skipped on docs-only changes), `cert-postgres-age.yml` and
`postgres-ignored.yml` run it as a final `always()` self-hosted-only step with
a 40 GiB `SELF_HOSTED_TARGET_CAP_BYTES`. A symlinked `target/debug` or swept
directory is refused (exit 2), `--max-bytes` must be positive (exit 3) and an
unreadable directory only warns. Tests: `scripts/test/test_ci_sweep_target.py`.
