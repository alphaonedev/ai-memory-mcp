**`agent_notified` webhook payloads carry one `correlation_id`, so body-echo receivers are acked (#4076).**
The `agent_notified` details block is flattened into the delivery envelope and
serialized the notification's own correlation handle (a `sha256:` digest of the
inbox row id) under the same top-level key as the envelope's delivery UUID. The
body therefore carried two `correlation_id` keys; a JSON parser keeps the last
one, so a receiver that followed the documented contract (parse the body, echo
`correlation_id`) acked the digest and the dispatcher rejected it as an ACK
mismatch, sending a healthy receiver's deliveries through the retry ladder to
the DLQ. The digest is now on the wire as `notification_correlation_id`; the
top-level `correlation_id` is only the delivery UUID, which the
`x-ai-memory-correlation-id` header and the audit row also carry. Consumers
that read the digest from the body must read `notification_correlation_id`.
The wake frames and the SSE stream are unchanged.
