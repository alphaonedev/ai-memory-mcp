**The compliance script-name gate reads a large document in linear time (#6352).**
`scripts/check_compliance_script_names.py` removed comments from the joined document with a
lazy regular expression, so each unclosed `<!--` searched the rest of the document for its
closer and a document of many unclosed openers took quadratic time. The document skeleton is
now one left-to-right scan: once a closer search fails every later opener is known to be
unclosed, and inline link destinations are parsed by a memoised scanner (nesting limited to
32 parentheses, as in CommonMark) instead of a backtracking pattern. A self-test cell bounds
five such inputs at under three seconds each.
