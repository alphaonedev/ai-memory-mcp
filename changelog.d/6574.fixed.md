The self-test of `scripts/claude-md-rule-compare.py` no longer aborts with a bare traceback when a cell outside the per-cell wrappers raises. A new top-level backstop (`guarded_run`) turns any exception that reaches it into the named line `FAIL: self-test - aborted in an unguarded cell: <type>: <message>` with exit 1. The parser-call cell now reports any exception as its own result, and the fixture-building cell groups (alias repository, merge-base, approval-range and the #6432 builders) run through `guarded()`, which names the group and continues with the remaining cells. Three new cells pin the parser-call result, `guarded()` and the backstop (#6574).
