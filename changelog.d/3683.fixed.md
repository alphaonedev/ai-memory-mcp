**`ai_memory_memories_refreshed_at_seconds` is absent until the first refresh instead of exporting 0 (#3683).**
The corpus gauge's freshness series was registered at construction with the
value `0`, so on a process whose refresher had not succeeded yet (the cold-prime
`COUNT` failed, or `AI_MEMORY_METRICS_GAUGE_REFRESH_SECS=0` before the first
scrape) the documented alert `time() - ai_memory_memories_refreshed_at_seconds`
read the series as 1970 and reported about 56 years of staleness, which trains
operators to mute it. The gauge is now registered on the first successful
publish, so the family is absent from `/metrics` until a refresh has happened,
then carries the real refresh time. A failed refresh still publishes nothing.
HELP text and the observability, API and architecture references now say
"absent until the first successful refresh".
