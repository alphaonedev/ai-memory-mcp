**Only encrypted data in transit — plaintext is now impossible to select (#3705).**

Operator mandate (2026-09-13): *"there is to never be any unencrypted data in
transit anywhere in the ai-memory architecture."* Five verified violations,
each now refused, loopback included:

1. **The daemon bound plaintext HTTP by default** — `AI_MEMORY_REQUIRE_TLS`
   was an opt-in defaulting OFF (fail-open) and the bind path served
   cleartext, `/metrics` included. Now a bind without `--tls-cert` +
   `--tls-key` is REFUSED on every host and the refusal names the plaintext
   path.
2. **The selector's grammar was narrower than its siblings** — `=yes` / `=on`
   silently left TLS optional. Now the ONE canonical grammar
   (`security_profile::is_truthy`) applies; a falsy token is a refused
   downgrade request and an unrecognised token REFUSES boot.
3. **Loopback was exempt everywhere** — webhook targets and federation peers
   accepted `http://` to loopback. Loopback is shared by every local process
   (*peer is loopback* is not *peer is trusted*, #2502); every `http://`
   target and peer is refused at create, at dispatch and at boot.
4. **A plaintext escape hatch existed** — `AI_MEMORY_FED_ALLOW_PLAINTEXT_PEERS`
   (and `AI_MEMORY_ALLOW_PLAINTEXT_NONLOOPBACK`) are REMOVED downgrade paths:
   a truthy value refuses boot in every posture.
5. **PostgreSQL transport encryption was checked, never enforced** —
   `sslmode=verify-full` is now a floor at the ONE connect funnel, for every
   deployment, refused before a socket opens.

Fail closed throughout (the deliberate opposite of #3701's fail-open
entitlement). New `src/transit_encryption.rs` is the single source of the
floor, the grammar and the refusals. `ai-memory doctor` gains "Transit
encryption (#3705)" as the THIRD section of its default report — the
pre-upgrade detector: selector token, armed downgrade paths, forward-URL
scheme, store DSN `sslmode`, stored plaintext webhook targets, plaintext
model-server egress (detected only; awaiting the operator's ruling) and the
boot verdict. Migration: the listener needs nothing (first boot generates
and renews a local certificate, #3709) unless you bring your own
`--tls-cert`/`--tls-key`; move peers and webhooks to `https://`; pin
`sslmode=verify-full` on the DSN — see `docs/SECURITY.md`.
