The janitor's torn-tail cut no longer depends on the journal lock alone (#6042). The cut truncated to a length read earlier without re-checking it, so the safety of the cut rested entirely on an advisory `flock`. The length is now re-checked against the descriptor immediately before the truncation, exactly as `_take_back` already does, and a journal that grew in between is refused rather than cut - so bytes another run has fsynced are never discarded even if the lock were ever wrong.
