The `store::postgres::tests::live_governance_*` lib tests (owner allow, non-owner deny, approve-level pending, inheritance cap) are no longer flaky under a parallel `cargo test --lib`: they set the process-global `PermissionsMode` to Enforce without the permissions-mode test gate, so another cell's Advisory window turned their expected Deny / Pending decisions into Allow (3 of 4 failed on every multi-thread run). They now take `lock_permissions_mode_for_test` before setting Enforce and hold it for the cell; assertions are unchanged (#4468).
