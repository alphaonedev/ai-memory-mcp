**`pg_write_gate_honors_cross_pool_stop_within_ttl_3276` measures its TTL window instead of assuming it (#6796).**
The within-TTL phase assumed the write after a cross-pool stop landed inside the 1 s
`RECORD_STOP_REFRESH_TTL_MS` window of the writer's cache clock. At host load ~100 the awaited Postgres
round-trips outlasted the window, the gate correctly re-derived the durable stop and refused, and the
test failed although production behaviour was right. The elapsed window is now measured when the write
is issued, the phase is redone once from a fresh connect when it was missed, and the assertion prints
the measured window. The past-TTL and fail-closed phases are unchanged.
