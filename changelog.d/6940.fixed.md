### Fixed

- **[ci-gate] cert-expiry self-test pins the five env sha read statements whole, and refuses a rebinding of `env`**
  ([#6940](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6940)).
  Cell (pr4-sha-ast) only checked that an `env_sha(env, KEY)` call existed somewhere in the module, so a conditional at the `PR_HEAD_SHA` read site that kept the call, an `env` rebound to a zero-width-space-stripped copy at the top of `resolve_range`, and a stripped copy passed from `run_gate` all survived `--self-test` (rc 0). The pin now compares each of the five read statements of `resolve_range` whole, counts the bindings of the names they assign, refuses any binding or mutation of `env` beyond the parameter of `resolve_range` and `run_gate`, and pins the `resolve_range(repo, env)` call in `run_gate`. Cell (pr4-sha-ast-mutants) applies the three mutants to a copy of the script and requires the pin to reject each. The "ANY character form" wording in the comment and in `changelog.d/6697.fixed.md` now states what the pin covers.
