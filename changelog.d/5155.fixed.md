**The lab probe-mutation matcher reads the whole log and names the knob with its trailing colon (#5155).** The bash matcher was fragile under `set -o pipefail` (a reader that closed early returned 141, turning a detection into "inconclusive") and its needle lacked the trailing colon, so a refusal for a longer knob name was counted. The matcher is now `probe_verdict` in `infra/federation-lab/run.py` (the Python port, #5815): it reads the whole log as bytes, with no pipe, and counts a line only when it holds `refuses to disable AI_MEMORY_REQUIRE_ROLLBACK_CHECK:` (the colon pins the whole knob name) and is not an `INFO` line. A log it cannot read is refused, never "not detected". The posture self-test has legs for both, including a log with 200000 lines before the refusal.
