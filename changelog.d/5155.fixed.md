**The lab probe-mutation matcher reads the whole log and names the knob with its trailing colon (#5155).** The matcher was fragile under `set -o pipefail` (a reader that closed early returned 141, turning a detection into "inconclusive") and its needle lacked the trailing colon, so a refusal for a longer knob name was counted. One awk pass now reads the log to EOF and the colon pins the whole knob name; the posture self-test has legs for both, including a large log.
