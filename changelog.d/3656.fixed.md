**`ai-memory doctor --remote` reads the daemon's `/health`, its scrape and peer freshness instead of stubbing four sections (#3656).**
Remote mode asked only `/capabilities` and `/stats`, so a daemon answering
`/health` with its fail-closed 503 (a cached FTS integrity verdict of `failed`)
still produced a clean report, and Index, Sync and Webhook were fixed "raw SQL"
N/A stubs. The report now leads with a `Health` section that reads the body at
every status, keeps the daemon's `ok` / `pending` / `stale` / `failed` /
`disabled` verdicts distinct and re-ages an `ok` verdict by the doctor's own
clock. Index reads `index_evictions_total` and `hnsw_size`, Storage reads
`dim_violations`, Webhook reads the delivery counters and DLQ overflow, and
Sync reads the DLQ, fanout counters and the per-peer freshness gauges
(`peers_failing`, `oldest_peer_success_age_secs`), never inferring convergence.
An absent series is `not_in_response`, never `0`. `/api/v1/stats` is
admin-only, so the global `--agent-id` is sent as `X-Agent-Id` and a 403 is
named in the report. The operator's `--remote` URL is rendered without userinfo
or query in every fact and note, and transport errors are classified rather
than carrying the request URL. Governance stays N/A with its reason.
