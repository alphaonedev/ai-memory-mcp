**A genesis-read skip counts and claims its retry backoff only once its append commits (#7746).** The skip counter, the `ai_memory_audit_watermark_genesis_read_skips_total` metric and the `GENESIS_SKIP_BACKOFF_SEQ` claim ride the `PendingAuditAnchor` and are applied by `emit_after_commit`, so a skip whose append rolls back neither counts nor suppresses the anchor of the re-appended sequence (the #7241 post-commit discipline, on sqlite and postgres). Inside one multi-append transaction the later appends are held back by a tentative marker until the commit applies the claim (#7755). Pinned by the sqlite cell and its postgres twin in `tests/audit_watermark_after_commit_7241.rs`.
