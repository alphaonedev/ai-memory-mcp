### Security

- Round 12 of `scripts/check-docs-no-argv-secrets.py`, per the 5-agent vote (4d3ea1c5): a waivable hit (`unknown-head-operand`, `array-element-operand`) now names the value kind of its flagged operand (`value=literal`, `placeholder`, `expansion` or `none`) and never prints the value; a quoted value (`pw='x'`, `pw="x"`) is masked like an unquoted one. A literal value, meaning any character left after removing expansions, quotes and backslashes (so `pw=abc$X` counts), can never be allowlisted: `scripts/regen-argv-secrets-allow.py` prints it as `REFUSED-LITERAL` with file, class, hash and masked preview, never writes it and exits 1 in every mode, and the gate refuses an allowlist line whose kind is `literal`. The allowlist format gains a value-kind field and the 16 entries were regenerated (none literal). (#5835)
