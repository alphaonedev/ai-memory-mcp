### Fixed

- **[ci-gate] cert-expiry sha pin refuses a second ENV_SHA_RE or env_sha binding and a changed production env build**
  ([#6882](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6882)).
  The (pr4-sha-ast) pin checked the first `ENV_SHA_RE` assignment and the `env_sha` function by name, so a second binding of either later in the module, or an env built differently in `main` before `run_gate`, loosened the sha validation without failing `--self-test`. The pin now requires exactly one binding of each of `ENV_SHA_RE` and `env_sha` in the module, the unchanged `run_gate(REPO_ROOT, dict(os.environ))` in `main`, and no other `os.environ` use there. Cell (pr4-sha-ast-mutants) adds the second `ENV_SHA_RE`, second `env_sha` and stripped-env-in-`main` mutants.
