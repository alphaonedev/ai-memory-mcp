### Security

- **[cli][tls] `serve --help` no longer promises a plaintext listener, and the
  references no longer defer the shipped `tls` verbs**
  ([#4006](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/4006)). The
  `--tls-cert` help said "Absent both flags = plain HTTP (same as every
  previous release)". Since #3705 `resolve_tls_material` uses material
  installed by `ai-memory tls import`, or generates local material for a
  singleton, and otherwise refuses to bind — it never serves plaintext. The
  help now says so. `docs/CLI_REFERENCE.md` and `docs/SECURITY.md` called
  `ai-memory tls init|import` v1.0.1 and routed rotation through an unshipped
  verb; v1.0.0 ships `tls init`, `tls import` and `tls status` (only `tls
  renew` and `db check-tls` are not shipped), and the fleet row now accepts
  operator material from the flags or `tls import`. Behaviour unchanged (the
  TLS-only default is the safer behaviour). Pinned by
  `tests/cli_serve_help_text.rs::serve_help_does_not_promise_plain_http_4006`
  and `tests/fit_docs_b_false_claims_4000_4009.rs`.
