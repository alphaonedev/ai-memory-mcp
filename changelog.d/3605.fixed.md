`backup` now writes its snapshot and manifest durably. The snapshot is
fsynced, the manifest is written to a temporary file and renamed into place,
and the directory is fsynced, so a power cut can no longer leave a snapshot
without a manifest, or a half-written manifest. `backup --json` reports
`durable`. A backup that could not be made durable is WARNed and rotates
nothing, so older backups are never deleted in its favour; under `asi-hard`
it exits non-zero.
