**The anti-cascade protections no longer ship OFF on a fleet (#3700).**

`AI_MEMORY_SECURITY_PROFILE=asi-hard` pins the machinery that stops one
agent's wrong conclusion from becoming a swarm's shared truth —
`REQUIRE_WITNESS` (corroboration), `REQUIRE_AGENT_ATTESTATION`,
`FED_QUARANTINE_UNATTRIBUTED`, the four `FED_REQUIRE_*_SIG` gates, cause
binding, identity lineage, rollback check, role separation, `CID_ENFORCE`,
`DB_SYNCHRONOUS` — and refuses to loosen any of it. Every one of those
shipped OFF: `standard` was the compiled default and nothing connected the
deployment's shape to its posture, so a federated multi-agent fleet booted
unprotected and silent.

- **The shape is the discriminator** (`src/deployment_shape.rs`). A
  deployment is fleet-shaped when any content-free signal is present:
  `outbound_peers` / `listener_mtls` (argv), `inbound_bindings` /
  `peer_allowlist` (env), `mcp_federation_forward_url` / `wake_hub`
  (config), or an `agent_registry` of two or more agents (store). A signal
  a process cannot see is `unobservable`, never `absent`.
- **Four states.** Fleet + selector unset → `asi-hard` DERIVED pre-runtime
  and pinned; with any knob below its floor the boot is REFUSED, naming
  every disabled knob and the one-line override
  `AI_MEMORY_SECURITY_PROFILE=standard` (a fleet learned only from the
  registry after the store opens refuses too, naming every protection off
  and both one-liners). Singleton + unset → `standard`, byte-identical.
  Explicit `standard` on a fleet → boots, warns once, RECORDED (forensic
  audit kind `deployment_shape.posture_exception`; capabilities
  `deployment_shape`). Explicit `asi-hard` → unchanged.
- **`doctor` says it unprompted.** "Deployment shape (#3700)" is the SECOND
  section of the default report: shape, posture, origin, whether they
  match, the protections that are off, and the boot verdict.
- **Migration honesty.** The detector ships with the refusal: run
  `ai-memory doctor` before upgrading to learn exactly what will refuse.
