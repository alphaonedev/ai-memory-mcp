**The authenticated monitoring routes now share the per-source auth-failure backoff (#4068).**
`/api/v1/monitoring/status` and `/api/v1/monitoring/metrics` are authenticated
by the outer monitoring gate rather than the transport gate, and that gate
neither consulted nor updated the #2502 per-source backoff. Wrong monitoring
credentials were never counted, a source already backed off on ordinary routes
was still admitted on monitoring, and with health-only monitoring scopes
configured the gate's own `401 unresolved_transport_principal` refusals were
not counted either. The gate now uses the same backoff table: it refuses a
backed-off source with `429` and `Retry-After` before looking at any key (a
correct key is refused too), records each failure, and resets the source on
success. Requests the gate does not authenticate are neither checked against
nor counted in the backoff: failures on the mTLS federation lane
(`/api/v1/sync/*`), which the transport gate also leaves out of backoff, stay a
plain `401` and cannot lock the peer address out of other routes, and the
plaintext-listener refusal of the monitoring routes stays `403`. The TLS
requirement, the scope rules and the public `/api/v1/health` liveness exemption
are unchanged.
