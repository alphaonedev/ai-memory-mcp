### Security

- **[storage] Snapshot and backup files are never written, chmod-ed or
  replaced through a planted name**
  ([#8950](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/8950)). The
  pre-migration snapshot, the doctor repair snapshot, `ai-memory backup` and
  the numbered config backups wrote or chmod-ed their final name BY NAME, and
  the publish could fall back to a replacing rename, so a symlink, a hard link
  or another file put at that name could be written through or replaced.
  - **One publish path** (`storage::snapshot_file`): the file is filled in a
    private (0700) staging directory, its staging name is checked against the
    open handle (device and inode), its mode is set through the handle, and it
    is published with a create-new hard link that never replaces or follows
    anything at the final name.
  - **Fail closed**: only a filesystem with no hard links (`EPERM`,
    `EOPNOTSUPP`, `ENOSYS`) falls back, and only to an atomic no-replace
    rename (Linux `renameat2(RENAME_NOREPLACE)`, macOS
    `renamex_np(RENAME_EXCL)`); every other link failure, and a platform with
    no such rename, refuses the publish.
  - **Numbered backups**: one create-new link per candidate name, at most
    1000 numbered names; a symlink at any candidate is refused.
