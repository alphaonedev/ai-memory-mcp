**The echo of a refusal row keeps only what precedes its first `: ` or `=` (#6783, carrier gate #6143).** The value part is withheld with its length, a row with no separator keeps its first 24 characters, and key and trigger names are echoed through `SUBSET.echo_name`; CLI-flag values, URL userinfo, header values, look-alike key spellings and percent-encoded tokens are no longer reprinted.
