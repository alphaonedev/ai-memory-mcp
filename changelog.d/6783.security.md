**The echo of a refusal row keeps only its key word (#6783, #6881, carrier gate #6143).** A refusal row keeps its indentation, an optional `- ` and one key word directly before its first `: `, tab, end-of-row colon or `=`; the rest of the row, and any row with no such key, is withheld with its length. CLI-flag values, URL userinfo, header values, look-alike key spellings and percent-encoded tokens are no longer reprinted, and key and trigger names are echoed through `SUBSET.echo_name`.
