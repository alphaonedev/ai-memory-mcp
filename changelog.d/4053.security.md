Postgres `update` (trait and If-Match) now applies an optional lifecycle transition inside the same owner-locked transaction as the content write, instead of in a second, owner-unaware transaction after commit. An ownership transfer can no longer land between the two writes and let the former owner change the new owner's row; an illegal transition now rolls the whole update back instead of refusing the call after its content had committed; and If-Match returns the version of the complete update (#4053, residual of #3953).
