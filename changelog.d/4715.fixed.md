governance bound (`GOVERNANCE_CHAIN_MAX_DEPTH`, 8 hops) and marks the

under such a chain. Pre-#4477 or imported data can still hold one. The census
reuses the `bind_chain_depth` walk from root segments only, on SQLite (the
Governance section) and PostgreSQL (new section "Governance chain depth
(#4715)", connected through the #3705 sslmode floor with the #4333 refusal
shape). The note names the offending namespaces (first 20, plus a count) and
the shortening hint. The doctor also stops swallowing read faults here: the
depth histogram and the governance coverage no longer fall back to an empty
value, and an unreadable `namespace_meta` row is a Critical finding.
