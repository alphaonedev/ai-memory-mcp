**Cert-expiry shadow scan splits on every YAML line break (#6228).**
The `GUARD SHADOW` scan of `scripts/check_cert_expiry.py` split workflow files on LF
only, while YAML parsers also start a new line at a lone carriage return (YAML 1.1 and
1.2) and at NEL, LS and PS (YAML 1.1 parsers such as PyYAML). A second job named for the
required cert-expiry context, joined by such a break onto a line of the exempt
`cert-expiry-gate` job of `c8-precheck.yml` or the `cert-expiry-trusted` job of the
trusted workflow, was read as one exempt line, so an approval trailer could turn the
trusted job green over a real second producer. The scan now splits on every YAML line
break (CRLF, CR, LF, NEL, LS, PS), so the hidden job is named, and a line break other
than LF or CRLF in any workflow file is itself refused as `GUARD SHADOW`, which no
trailer waives. CRLF files are unaffected. Self-test cells plant the shape with CR,
U+2028, U+2029 and NEL in each of the two own files (RED with the trailer) and pin each YAML-hazard
rule of the own files with an isolated cell. Refs #6140.
