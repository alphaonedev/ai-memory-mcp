**zip-extract guard sees `paste!` pieces (#7307).** The guard joins every `[< .. >]` group in a scanned source file (whatever the macro is named or aliased) and fails when the joined name, compared without case or underscores, is a candle npy/npz/pth loader, so `Tensor::[<read_ npz>]` and `[<READ_NPZ:lower>]` no longer slip past the loader regex.
