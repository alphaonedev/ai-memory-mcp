Restrict `memory_signal_ack` to the signal's addressee. Any agent could
previously acknowledge a signal addressed to another agent — which also hid it
from the real recipient's unacked-only inbox — and the `coordination.signal_ack`
audit row was attributed to the signal's `to_agent`, so the tamper-evident
record named the victim rather than the acknowledger. The caller identity is now
resolved from the MCP session (never a body field), a non-addressee or
namespace-broadcast acknowledgement is refused without touching the row, and the
audit row records the resolved caller.
