**The env-mutation gate treats an argument handed to a same-file spawning helper as detached (#8044).** `fn bg<F>(f: F) { std::thread::spawn(f); }` followed by `bg(closure)`, `bg(var)` or `bg(fn_item)` under the lock ran the work outside the guard, but the gate let it inherit the lock; a fn that hands a parameter to a spawn, directly or through another such fn, is now a detaching helper.
