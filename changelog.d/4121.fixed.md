**`ai-memory doctor` reachability probes go through inference-egress
admission and the endpoint pin (#4121).** The LLM and embeddings
reachability probes built their own HTTP client and never consulted
`AI_MEMORY_INFERENCE_EGRESS`: under `deny` (or `loopback-only` /
`internal-only` against an off-host endpoint) they still opened a connection
and sent the configured API key. Both probes now obtain their client from
`egress::admitted_blocking_client`, which admits the target BEFORE any client
exists, pins the resolved addresses with proxies off under `internal-only`,
and never follows a redirect. A refused probe sends nothing and reports a
`warning` section naming the posture, the endpoint origin (never the full
URL) and the refusal reason, with `inference_egress` and
`inference_egress_mode` facts. `allow` probes exactly as before. Pinned by
`tests/doctor_probe_egress_admission_4121.rs` (a recording listener proves
zero connections and no key bytes on every refused cell) with a structural
leg that refuses a hand-built `Client::builder()` in either probe.
