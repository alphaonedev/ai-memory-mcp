**#5076 — the prepared-statement caveat in `docs/enterprise-deployment.md` section 5.6.6 now names every failure mode.** sqlx names its statements `sqlx_s_<n>` from a counter that restarts on every client connection, so clients that share one server connection without prepared-statement tracking can hit "does not exist" or "already exists" errors, or run a same-named statement another client prepared. The caveat records this as a further reason the pooler stays in `session` mode (with `server_reset_query = DISCARD ALL`) until #4679 lands.
