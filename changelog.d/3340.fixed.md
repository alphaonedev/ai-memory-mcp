**`GET /api/v1/capabilities` tells the truth about atomisation on a
postgres-backed daemon (#3340).** The `atomisation` capability was reported
backend-blind — every engine sub-feature (`tool`, `cli`, `auto`, `curator`)
read `implemented` — while the engine itself is sqlite-only: on postgres
every store reports `atomise_outcome=skipped_backend_unsupported` and the
HTTP atomise route refuses. On a postgres daemon those four fields now read
`unsupported_on_postgres`, with an additive `unsupported_on_postgres: true`
flag and an `unsupported_reason` naming the failure mode, mirroring the
skills-plane overlay; the read/label surfaces (`recall_preference`,
`forensic`, `link_relation`) and the sqlite response are unchanged.
