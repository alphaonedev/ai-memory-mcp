### Fixed

- **[ci][gate] Rule (f) of `check-required-contexts.sh` now derives its scope
  from workflow triggers** ([#5331](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/5331)).
  Rule (f) previously audited only the five workflows in a hardcoded list, so
  seven pull-request-triggered workflows (15 jobs, including `token-budget.yml`
  and `tool-count-drift.yml`) could carry a gate that was declared nowhere. Scope
  is now every workflow triggering on `pull_request`, `pull_request_target` or
  `merge_group`, minus the dated exclusion ledger
  `scripts/qc-allowlists/required-contexts-scope-excluded.txt` (stale,
  absent-file and malformed rows hard-fail; a workflow with no parseable trigger
  fails closed). The 15 newly in-scope jobs are declared dated not-required
  pending the operator-sequenced #3554 lockstep. `COVERED_WORKFLOWS` remains,
  byte-identical, as the release attribution carrier set.
