### Security

- **Curator reflections keep the visibility of their sources**
  ([#8850](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/8850)). The reflection pass
  read its source observations with an admin context, so it could cluster rows the curator
  identity was not allowed to see, and it stamped every derived reflection `collective`, which
  republished private or team rows to every reader. Reads now use the curator's own agent
  context; a reflection carries the most restrictive scope of its sources
  (`visibility::narrowest_scope`), recomputed from a fresh read just before the write; a source
  that is missing or not readable at persist time refuses the write with an exact error
  (fail closed). Test: `tests/curator_reflection_visibility_8850.rs` (SQLite and Postgres).
