**A sqlite audit append now fails closed when the genesis row cannot be read at a watermark-emission sequence (#7238, #7239).** It used to log a WARN, skip the watermark and commit the append, while postgres aborted. Both backends now propagate the read error; the decision is recorded at both call sites and pinned by `sqlite_genesis_read_error_fails_the_append_closed_7239` and its postgres twin.
