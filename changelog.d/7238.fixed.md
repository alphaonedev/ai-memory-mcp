**A genesis read error at a watermark-emission sequence skips the anchor instead of failing the audit append, on both backends (#7238, #7239, #7604).** The skip is WARNed and counted, no db_id-less watermark is written, the append succeeds so the chain never stalls and best-effort callers keep their audit row, and the next emission-due append retries. Postgres reads the genesis row inside a SAVEPOINT so the error cannot abort the append's transaction. Decided by 3-agent vote (6def5ab6), 3-0; pinned by `sqlite_genesis_read_error_skips_the_watermark_and_the_append_succeeds_7239` and its postgres twin.
