Hardening batch for the red-team LOW findings in #3204 (items 3, 4, 5 and 7
had already landed with #3178, #3171, #3604 and #3383; this closes the rest).

- `GET /api/v1/memories/{id}` no longer discloses the ids and relations of
  other tenants' rows: an edge rides the `links` array only when its far
  endpoint is readable by the caller, on both backends (the same filter
  `GET /api/v1/links/{id}` already applied). Before, the anchor was
  visibility-checked and every linked private row of every other agent was
  enumerated by id.
- Signed signals and attested checkpoint resolutions now commit a
  domain-separation tag (`ai-memory/signal/v1`,
  `ai-memory/checkpoint-resolution/v1`), closing the two contexts #1931 left
  untagged. **Breaking, fail-closed like #1931:** a signal or resolution
  signed by a pre-3204 binary no longer verifies (`memory_signal_read`
  reports `verified: false`; a strict-mode receiver skips the relayed
  signal; the K1-pinned newest witness / role / governance-verdict anchor
  reports `Forged` or is withheld until the next anchor is emitted under
  the tagged encoding — the next watermark, governed verdict, graceful
  shutdown, or `ai-memory audit re-anchor`). Upgrade federated peers in
  lockstep, as #1931 required.
- Federation refusal bodies (`/sync/push`, `/sync/since`, the #2045 cert
  binding, the #238 envelope gate, FED-RQ-03) no longer echo the
  environment knob that disables the control that fired; the closed-set
  `note` says what was refused and the operator remediation rides the
  refusal-site WARN as a structured `remediation` field.
- Documented: the federation trust bundle is read once per daemon
  process, so an issuer revocation needs a restart until FED-P3.
