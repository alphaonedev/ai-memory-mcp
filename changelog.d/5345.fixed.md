**The doc symbol-anchor gate now range-checks a `src/x.rs:2--9999` anchor and one that uses the minus sign U+2212.** The `PATHLN` separator accepted a single hyphen, an en dash and an em dash, so a doubled hyphen or a minus sign made the anchor read as a single line and a range past end-of-file passed.
