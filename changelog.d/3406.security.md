HTTP `capture_turn` now applies the HTTP-direct attestation posture: unsigned
captures are refused by default, and explicit permissive opt-outs are audited.
Signed captures must pass both host-key enrollment and the resolved caller's
bound-key check on SQLite and PostgreSQL. Legacy host signatures remain
`signed_by_peer`, with durable session/turn deduplication providing idempotency;
they do not attest the namespace, identity or timestamp as a `SignableWrite`.
