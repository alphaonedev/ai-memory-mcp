**Federation DLQ bookkeeping failures are now handled, counted and named (#3658).**
The push-DLQ replay worker discarded the result of every local bookkeeping
write (`bump_dlq_attempt`, `note_dlq_throttled`, and the `mark_dlq_row_replayed`
clears were only logged), and a failed DLQ enqueue was only logged, so a
broken local DLQ store looked exactly like a failing peer: attempt budgets
and `last_error` froze and rows could never reach quarantine. Every such
write now goes through one handler that logs at `error` with row, peer, op
and error, increments the new closed-label counter
`ai_memory_federation_push_dlq_bookkeeping_failed_total{op}`
(`bump_attempt`, `note_throttled`, `mark_replayed`, `reset_throttled`,
`enqueue`), and records it on the `ReplayTick` that `replay_once` now
returns. The behaviour on failed persistence is defined: the tick continues,
the row keeps its pre-tick budget and is retried next tick. Refs #6049.
