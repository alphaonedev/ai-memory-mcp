**`check-docs-vs-ssot.sh` now checks a steps-arrow claim written with a doubled space or a tab (#5140, #5584, #5585).** The steps anchor required exactly one space before the FROM version, so a claim spaced differently was never matched and could never fail, which let a stale schema-version claim pass. The anchor now spells its whitespace as spaces: one or more after `steps`, any number (including none) around the arrow. Every view the anchors see is whitespace-folded first, so a tab, a doubled space and a non-breaking space already arrive as one space, and a space-only anchor sees what an any-whitespace one saw; the marker fold can still leave a run of spaces, which the anchor accepts. The self-test plants 52 (rejected) and 53 (accepted) in the doubled-space and tab forms and pins the spacing: a run of spaces left by a marker before the FROM version or on either side of the arrow (rejected), no space after `steps` (accepted) and no space around the arrow (rejected). Reverting the anchor to the any-whitespace spelling changes no verdict, because the fold runs first; that equivalence is recorded at the anchor (#5650).
