**`check-docs-vs-ssot.sh` now checks a steps-arrow claim written with a doubled space or a tab (#5140).** The steps anchor required exactly one space before the FROM version, so a claim spaced differently was never matched and could never fail, which let a stale schema-version claim pass. The anchor now accepts any run of spaces or tabs (including none); the self-test plants 52 (rejected) and 53 (accepted) in the doubled-space and tab forms.
