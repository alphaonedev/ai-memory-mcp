Federation `/sync/push` on a PostgreSQL-backed receiver no longer charges the author's cumulative storage-bytes quota for a signal it already stores. A redelivered signal (lost response, partial-batch retry) is now detected before the charge and acknowledged as a no-op, as on SQLite, and a charge that bought no storage (a concurrent duplicate or a failed write) is refunded exactly on both backends. Previously each redelivery grew a counter that never resets until the author's namespace allowance was exhausted by one stored row. Same-ID memory merges that change nothing are not yet refunded; that dimension of the issue remains open (#4026).
