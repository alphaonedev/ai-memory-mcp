**`doctor_dim_violations` no longer reports a read fault as `0` or "pre-P2 schema" (#4981, WP-FAULT #6051).**
Only a missing `embedding_dim` column means pre-P2 (`Ok(None)`); any other
prepare or census failure (an unreadable `memories` table, a corrupt schema)
is an error. The doctor Storage section renders `dim_violations = unreadable`
with a `dim_violations_error` fact at **Critical** instead of
`not_observed (pre-P2 schema)` at Info.
