### Fixed

- **[test-hygiene] Live-PG covenant env knobs and sever-audit count
  assertions raced at `--test-threads=4`**
  ([#3593](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3593)).
  Test-only; no product bytes.

  `tests/issue_2059_2060_covenant_pg.rs` mutates `AI_MEMORY_REQUIRE_WHY_TRACE`
  / `AI_MEMORY_REQUIRE_IMMUTABLE_AUTHORSHIP` from 16 tests, but only the two
  in-memory sqlite cases took `covenant_env_lock`. Unique namespaces isolate
  rows, not the process-global knobs, so a postgres sibling could `remove_var`
  while `sqlite_system_principal_exempt_tenant_gated_under_enforce_2110` was
  asserting tenant refusal. All 18 tests now hold the existing tokio
  mutex across `.await` (16 mutators + 2 readers; CONCURRENCY-20) and
  mutators go through a `CovenantEnvGuard` that restores on Drop,
  including panic (OWNERSHIP-24, UNSAFE-01).

  `tests/pg_sever_audit_event_3290.rs` asserted `COUNT(*)` / newest-row over
  `event_type` alone. Unique namespaces do not isolate that query. A module
  tokio mutex now serialises the setup / before-count / reap / after-count /
  newest-event / cleanup window. Tenant-denied / system-allowed assertions,
  exact sever deltas, daemon attribution, and chain assertions are unchanged.
