**The env-mutation gate treats every closure literal that is not provably on the same thread as detached (#8056).** A closure written inside `Box::new`, `Some`, a struct field or `vec![..]` and then spawned was credited with the lock; only an in-place call, a plain `let` that is only called, a same-thread closure's returned value, or the argument of a known non-spawning callee keep the lock.
