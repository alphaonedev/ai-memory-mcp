### Fixed

- **[ci-gate] cert-expiry self-test now pins the 64-hex (SHA-256 object format) sha form**
  ([#6144](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6144)).
  `scripts/check_cert_expiry.py` accepts a 40- or 64-hex sha from `PR_HEAD_SHA`,
  `GITHUB_SHA`, `PR_BASE_SHA` and (push events) `GITHUB_EVENT_BEFORE`, but no self-test
  cell pinned the 64-hex form, so narrowing the validator regex to 40 hex still passed.
  (`CERT_EXPIRY_BASE` / `CERT_EXPIRY_HEAD` are refs honoured outside CI only and are not
  hex-validated.) New cells assert, on both the `pull_request` lane (`PR_HEAD_SHA`, `GITHUB_SHA`) and the
  `push` lane (`GITHUB_EVENT_BEFORE`, `GITHUB_SHA`), that a 64-hex sha is accepted by the
  validator (and fails cleanly at the later lookup), that upper-case 40/64-hex shas are
  accepted (the validator is case-insensitive), and that 63/65-hex shas are refused by the
  validator with only the `git --version` probe traced: the test shim records every git
  invocation's argv, so a rev-parse or fetch run ahead of the validator turns the self-test
  red on either lane.
  Mutant check: narrowing the regex to 40 hex or to lower-case hex, narrowing the push
  `GITHUB_EVENT_BEFORE` or `GITHUB_SHA` site, or adding a silent pre-validator `rev-parse`
  or `fetch` on either lane, now turns the self-test red.
