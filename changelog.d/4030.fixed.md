### Fixed

- **CRDT merge: the merged row's clock can no longer regress below its content**
  ([#4030](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/4030)). Content LWW compared parsed
  instants while the merged `updated_at` compared raw RFC3339 strings, so a non-UTC offset
  (`10:00+02:00` = 08:00Z) could be stamped over newer `09:00Z` content, letting a later stale edit
  win. Every timestamp the merge orders (`updated_at`, `created_at`, `last_accessed_at`,
  `expires_at`) now uses one instant order (µs, the postgres resolution); winners keep their
  original bytes.
