**PR comparison masking, round 6 (#6851).** `scripts/claude-md-rule-compare.py` masked the password of a URL only when the host after the `@` was ASCII letters, digits, dots and hyphens followed by a port, path, query, fragment, space or the end of the line (a round-5 regression). The password is now masked whatever follows the host: a backtick, parenthesis, quote, angle bracket, comma or semicolon, an IPv6 literal, an underscore, a percent escape or a non-ASCII host.
