### Security

- **[redaction] `config show --effective` no longer echoes a schemeless `db` credential**
  ([#6102](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6102)). A `db` / `store_url` value
  without `://` (a libpq key/value DSN, `postgres:/user:pw@host/db`) was printed verbatim; it now
  renders through the store-URL funnel, and a value that may carry a credential shows as
  `<redacted-db-path: '@' or '=' may delimit a credential>` (the marker states why the path is
  not shown). A value of ANY `scheme:` with a userinfo `@` renders through the URL funnel, so an
  unknown scheme fails closed rather than printing verbatim.
