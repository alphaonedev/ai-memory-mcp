### Security

- **[sdk-python] TLS verification is enforced at use time on an SDK-owned context**
  ([#6249](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6249),
  [#6267](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6267),
  [#6306](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6306)). `AiMemoryClient` and
  `AsyncAiMemoryClient` no longer hand the caller's `verify=` object to `httpx`. Every client builds
  its own private, frozen TLS context, copies the CA certificates (and minimum TLS version) from a
  caller's `ssl.SSLContext` or `truststore.SSLContext`, loads `cert=` into it, and re-checks
  `CERT_REQUIRED` plus hostname checking under a lock around every handshake. A caller context
  weakened after construction, a truststore context with verification off, or a concurrent
  `verify_mode` flip can no longer reach a handshake. `verify=False`, contexts with verification or
  hostname checking off, CRL-checking contexts, contexts with no copyable CA certificate, CA paths
  that are missing, unreadable, larger than 16 MiB or neither a file nor a directory, and any
  unclassifiable `verify=` or `cert=` value raise `ValueError` at construction.
