A federated pending-action approval received over `/sync/push` is now completable. Both receive funnels (SQLite and PostgreSQL) run the approved action's effect before the approval becomes durable and commit the approval (and a consensus threshold's final vote) only after the effect landed; on PostgreSQL the pending row is held `FOR UPDATE` across the unit. A failed or interrupted execution therefore leaves the action `pending`, so a redelivery of the same decision retries it, and a redelivery of an already completed approval is acknowledged as a no-op instead of being refused as "already decided". Previously a failed execution left an `approved` row whose effect was missing and could never be completed (#4025).

The unit now also stamps a durable execution marker (a reserved `payload` key, no schema change) once the effect has landed and before the approval commits. A pending row that carries the marker cannot be rejected or expired over its applied effect, and a redelivered approval completes it without re-running the effect. A redelivered approval of an `approved` row that has no marker (approved before the marker existed, or through a local surface whose execution failed) is acknowledged but reported, and `ai-memory doctor` counts such rows and warns on both backends (#4345). The effect and the approval are still not one transaction; that, and the SQLite cross-process double effect, stay tracked in #4346.
