**The env-mutation gate fails closed on non-canonical closure carriers into a spawn (#8051).** A closure carried to `spawn` by `Box::new(f)`, `f.clone()`, `Some(f)`, a struct field, a `for` binding or a wrapping closure was credited with the spawner's lock; carriers are now followed through their bindings and an untraceable one flags every closure of the fn, never credits it.
