**`test_auto_atomise_does_not_block_store_response` no longer trips on a loaded host (#6793).**
The on-path latency check asserted a fixed 50 ms ceiling and failed at 61 ms under host load ~85
although the hook was not blocking. The ceiling is now `max(50 ms, 20 x baseline)` where the baseline
is a per-run median of bare `thread::spawn` + `join` round trips; a blocking hook (a curator
round-trip) still fails, a slow runner does not. The 10x on/off ratio bound is unchanged.
