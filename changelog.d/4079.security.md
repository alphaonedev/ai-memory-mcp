Postgres-backed `DELETE /api/v1/subscriptions` and `DELETE /api/v1/memories/{id}` now fan the deletion out to federation peers through the quorum delete lane (push-DLQ backed), so a withdrawn webhook subscription stops dispatching from peer replicas and deleted rows no longer persist on peers; a quorum miss returns the W3/G12 `202` with replication state (#4079, #4153).
