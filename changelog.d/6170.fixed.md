**The compliance script-name gate now requires an allowlisted document to carry its own erratum (#6170).**
Previously the gate collected errata from all of `docs/compliance/` into one map, so
deleting the erratum line from `BALLOT-PROCEDURE.md` alone stayed green while the
standard's erratum still covered the allowlist pair. An allowlisted
`<doc>:<name>` pair in `scripts/qc-allowlists/compliance-script-names-allow.txt` is
now honoured only while that document itself carries an erratum line for `<name>`.
The two documents that cannot carry one (the SHA-256 pinned
`v1.0.0-DECLARATION.md` and `ENTERPRISE-FEDERATION-CERTIFICATION.md`, guarded by the
cert section 7 gate) are marked `:pinned` and stay honoured while an erratum for the
name exists anywhere in `docs/compliance/`. `:pinned` is a closed set of exactly
those two documents, enforced by the gate (#6173).
