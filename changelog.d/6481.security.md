**The carrier verifier reads `c8-precheck.yml` through the fail-closed YAML-subset reader instead of regexes (#6481, #6482, #6483, #6542, #6543, #6544, #6545, carrier gate #6143).** `on_events`, `forbidden_triggers` and `job_token_problems` now work on the parsed tree. Quoted, escaped and respelled trigger keys, workflow-level `env`/`defaults`/`permissions` secrets, `needs` outputs, aliases, flow and quoted env keys, case-variant or duplicate job ids, and non-ASCII space or line-break characters before a comment are caught or refused with the construct and line. The `permissions` pin was incomplete here (a flow mapping, a block scalar or `read-all` passed, and an absent block was not flagged); #6610 closes it. `workflow_call` joins the forbidden triggers, and a multi-document or duplicate-key workflow fails closed. Token comparison is pinned by self-test cells; the round-6 review mutant campaign found three surviving mutants (case-sensitive `secrets`, case-insensitive `GH_TOKEN`, a dead `permissions` test variant), closed by #6619 and #6620.
