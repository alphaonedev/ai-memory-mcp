**The carrier verifier reads `c8-precheck.yml` through the fail-closed YAML-subset reader instead of regexes (#6481, #6482, #6483, #6542, #6543, #6544, #6545, carrier gate #6143).** `on_events`, `forbidden_triggers` and `job_token_problems` now work on the parsed tree. Quoted, escaped and respelled trigger keys, workflow-level `env`/`defaults`/`permissions` secrets, `needs` outputs, aliases, flow and quoted env keys, case-variant or duplicate job ids, and non-ASCII space or line-break characters before a comment are caught or refused with the construct and line. `workflow_call` joins the forbidden triggers, and a multi-document or duplicate-key workflow fails closed. Token comparison is pinned by cells and a stdlib mutant campaign.
