**The live SAL consolidator now honours the curator's rollback-log halt and op budget (#3170).**
With `[curator.compaction].enabled = true` the SAL `ConsolidationPass` is the
live destructive consolidator, but it ran after the autonomy passes without
looking at their `rollback_log_degraded` flag and without any share of
`max_ops_per_cycle`, so the #3116 fail-closed halt and hard op cap applied only
to the consolidator that was switched off. The curator now skips the SAL pass
(with a reported reason) when the cycle's rollback log is already degraded,
hands it only the remaining cycle budget (one op per summarised cluster; clusters
past the budget are deferred to the next cycle and counted), charges its ops
back to the cycle, and folds its own rollback write-ahead failures into the
cycle's `rollback_log_degraded` flag. The store-backed sweep is capped at
`max_ops_per_cycle` the same way: it now gathers candidates with the same
`max_ops_per_cycle * 4` cap as the sqlite path (previously the candidate list
itself was capped at `max_ops_per_cycle`, which formed too few clusters for the
budget ever to apply), and clusters past the budget are deferred to the next
cycle. Refs #6055, #3116.
