**An expired offloaded blob is refused on `memory_deref` (#3392).** `ttl_seconds`
was honoured only by the write-side reaper: the read path never selected the
TTL and never checked it, so a customer who set a TTL to meet a retention
obligation could have the blob served verbatim after it passed, to a caller
who believed it was gone. The authenticated read path now reads the TTL and
refuses an expired blob with the same leak-resistant `not found` the
ownership gate uses. Substrate-internal callers (the TTL sweeper, the
integrity audit, the operator dump) still reach the row to reap or inspect
it, so nothing becomes uncleanable; a blob with no TTL, or one whose TTL has
not passed, is served exactly as before.
