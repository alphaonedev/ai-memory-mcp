**An expired offloaded blob is refused on `memory_deref` (#3392).** `ttl_seconds`
was honoured only by the write-side reaper: the read path never selected the
TTL and never checked it, so a customer who set a TTL to meet a retention
obligation could have the blob served verbatim after it passed, to a caller
who believed it was gone. Every read path — the MCP `memory_deref` and the
operator CLI `ai-memory deref` alike — now reads the TTL and refuses an
expired blob with the same leak-resistant `not found` the ownership gate
uses. The daily sweep deletes expired rows on its own and never reads
through this path, so nothing becomes uncleanable; a blob with no TTL, or
one whose TTL has not passed, is served exactly as before.
