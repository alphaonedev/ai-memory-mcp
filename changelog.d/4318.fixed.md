**A failing audit log no longer floods stderr through `tracing` (#4318, #4310).** When the flat audit trail (#3975) or the forensic log (#4302) could not write, each lost event also logged a `tracing` ERROR, and `serve` and `mcp` send tracing to stderr. That was one stderr line per lost event, alongside the rate-limited diagnostic meant to prevent exactly that. The per-event line is now DEBUG, and the ERROR goes out only with the rate-limited diagnostic (at most once a minute, with the count of events lost since). `ai_memory_audit_write_failures_total` and `ai_memory_forensic_sink_unavailable_total` still count every loss.
