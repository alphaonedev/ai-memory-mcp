**The compliance script-name gate tracks markup after a name fragment to its close across the whole paragraph (#6753, #6757).**
`scripts/check_compliance_script_names.py` read only a fragment's own line and the next line, so a comment,
tag or link title that closed two or more lines later passed while GitHub rendered the joined stale name.
The join walk now reads the rest of the paragraph (up to the next blank line, at most 512 characters) and
tracks each comment, tag or link from its opener to its close. A construct that does not close within the
paragraph, or whose close may lie past the 512-character cap, is reported as undecidable instead of passing.
