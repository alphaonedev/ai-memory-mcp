**CI: self-hosted runners prune their own stale `TMPDIR` at job start (#8634).**
Tests leave databases, WAL files and backups under the runner `TMPDIR`, and
nothing removed them: on one node four runner directories held 771 GB and a GA
test job was refused at the disk floor. `scripts/ci/prune_runner_tmp.py` now
removes direct entries of the runner's own `TMPDIR` whose mtime is older than
one day (a symlink is unlinked, never followed; a sibling runner directory is
never touched). It refuses, failing the job with the reason on stderr, when
`TMPDIR` is unset, relative, a symlink, not owned by the user, a forbidden path
or outside the allowed roots; a failure on one entry is counted, not fatal. The
composite action `.github/actions/prune-runner-tmp` runs it as the step right
after checkout in every self-hosted job. Tests:
`scripts/ci/tests/test_prune_runner_tmp_8634.py`.
