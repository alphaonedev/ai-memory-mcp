### Security

- **[redaction] Federation and webhook URL renderers fail closed on an ambiguous authority**
  ([#6101](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6101)). `url_origin` and
  `url_origin_and_path` rendered a URL whose userinfo held an unencoded `/`, `?`, `#` or `\` as host
  plus path, so the password reappeared in logs, doctor notes and audit lines. A parsed URL with a
  literal `@` after its authority now renders as `<scheme>://<redacted-authority>`, and sync refuses
  such a peer because its URL has no unique `sync_state` key.
