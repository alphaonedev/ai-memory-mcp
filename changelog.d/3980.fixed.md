**A webhook delivery queued behind a dispatch slot survives a crash as a `pending` audit row (#3980).**
A delivery's `subscription_events` audit row was written by its worker, which
runs only once the delivery holds a dispatch permit. #3979 records a delivery
still queued at the graceful drain deadline to the DLQ, but a crash (SIGKILL,
an OOM kill, an abort) never reaches the drain, so every admitted delivery that
had not started vanished with no audit row and no DLQ row. The audit row is now
written at admission, as `delivery_status = "pending"` with the full payload,
before any worker is spawned: on the sqlite path through the dispatching
caller's own connection inside one SAVEPOINT per dispatch call, on a postgres
daemon through the sqlite audit sidecar. The worker only settles the row. A row
that cannot be written fails closed: that delivery is routed to the DLQ and not
sent. On a drain miss the shutdown sweep replaces the unstarted delivery's
`pending` row with its `shutdown_unstarted` DLQ row in one transaction, so a
delivery still has exactly one durable record. After a crash the unstarted
deliveries are returned by `memory_subscription_replay` and counted as stale
pending rows by `ai-memory doctor`; nothing re-sends them automatically, because
a restart cannot tell an unsent delivery from one that was sent just before the
crash. Design by 5-agent vote (4-1).
