The Plan-C operator upgrade recipe (`infra/plan-c/docker-compose.yml` and `docs/plan-c-deployment.md`) uses `up -d --build --force-recreate` and preserves the signing-key, audit and TLS volumes. The runbook no longer says `down -v`: that flag deletes the key volumes, so each daemon regenerates a NEW random keypair under the same identity and enrolled peers refuse it. Volume deletion is reserved for deliberate destructive full teardown.
