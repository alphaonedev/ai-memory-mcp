CI footprint on the linux-fed self-hosted hosts (#7188): the enterprise-fed leg now builds into a per-runner `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` (`~/.cache/ai-memory-ci/cargo-target/<runner name>`, `scripts/ci/shared_cargo_target.py`, fails closed on a missing or unsafe runner name) instead of a ~166 GiB target per checkout, so a target has exactly one writer and compiles once per runner; the dev/test profile strips dependency debuginfo (`[profile.dev.package."*"]`, profile sections only, no dependency change); a post-job `scripts/ci/prune_runner_artifacts.py --protected-root` removes stale scratch without touching the per-runner target root; and `strategy.max-parallel: 3` caps the `check` matrix. The host budget formula (`labelled-runners x per-runner target`, ~158 GiB measured after the strip, a lower bound) is in `docs/DEVELOPER_SETUP_NOTES.md`; disk capacity is tracked in #7199.
