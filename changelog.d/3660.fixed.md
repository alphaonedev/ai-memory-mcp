**#3660 — the read-gate audit-append WARN no longer claims "DLQ-backed".**

When a `read_action` rule is engaged and the `signed_events` append for the
read decision fails, `gate_read` logs and lets the read proceed. The WARN
line and the doc/inline comments said the failure was "DLQ-backed" and that
"the deferred-audit DLQ keeps the trail recoverable". Nothing on that path
spools, queues or retries the append. The WARN now says the decision is NOT
recorded in `signed_events` and NOT queued, spooled or retried, and that only
the best-effort forensic emit (if enabled) may hold it. Wording only — the
read-availability posture is unchanged; counters and a strict knob stay with
the #3660 follow-up.
