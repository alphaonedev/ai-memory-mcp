**The env-mutation gate treats a fn item passed to spawn and a spawning macro as detached (#8038).** `std::thread::spawn(work)` never called `work` with parentheses, so its reader was never judged, and `bg!(move || { .. })` over a `macro_rules!` that spawns kept the caller's lock. A same-file fn named as the spawn argument is reached with an empty context, and the arguments of a spawning macro are detached spans. Test-infrastructure only.
