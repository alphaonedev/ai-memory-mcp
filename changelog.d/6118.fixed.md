**Self-hosted runners no longer accumulate test executables; one debuginfo level per fleet `target/` (#6118).**
One full `cargo test` build linked ~1000 test executables (~170 MB each at
`line-tables-only`) into each self-hosted runner's persistent `target/debug/deps`
and no step removed them: two linux-fed runners each held 164 GB and took the
f2 root filesystem from 224 GB to 78 GB free in two hours. Every self-hosted
cargo job (`ci.yml` `check`, `cert-postgres-age.yml`, `postgres-ignored.yml`,
`session-boot-lifetime.yml`) now ends with `Prune runner target dir (#6118)`
under `if: always()`, running the new `scripts/ci/prune-runner-target.py`
(Python 3.9+, stdlib): it deletes the test/example executables, their `.d` /
`.dSYM` companions and `incremental/`, keeps the rlib / rmeta / proc-macro,
`build/` and `.fingerprint/` warm cache, fails closed on anything that is not a
cargo target dir, never follows a symlink, and prints `freed_bytes=<n>`
(`--dry-run`, `--scope all` for the disk-emergency wipe). The same four jobs
declare `CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG` and `CARGO_PROFILE_TEST_DEBUG` = `"0"`: the
level is part of cargo's artifact hash, so the three previous levels
(`line-tables-only`, `"0"`, cargo's default) kept three complete artifact trees
per runner, and `line-tables-only` was already in force when the 164 GB was
written. Measured (sandbox): an integration test binary is 484 MB at cargo's
default, 130 MB at `line-tables-only` and 11.6 MB at `0` (11x). Pinned by
`scripts/test/test_ci_runner_target_hygiene_6118.py` (Section G of
`scripts/test/test-ci-workflow-invariants.sh`); runbook §3.3 of
`docs/DEV-CI-ENVIRONMENT.md`.
