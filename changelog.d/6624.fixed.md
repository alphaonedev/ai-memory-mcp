### Fixed

- **[ci-gate] cert-expiry banner Binds-to sha refuses anything but exactly 40 or 64 hex**
  ([#6624](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6624)).
  `scripts/check_cert_expiry.py` captured the first 40 hex characters of the Binds-to value with no right boundary, so a 41- to 63-hex, 64-hex or 65+-hex value was cut to 40 characters instead of refused. The capture is now exactly 40 or 64 hex followed by a negative lookahead; self-test cell (x3) pins 41, 63 and 65 hex refused and 40 and 64 hex parsed whole.
