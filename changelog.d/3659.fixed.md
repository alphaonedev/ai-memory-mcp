**Webhook delivery-audit bookkeeping failures no longer disappear (#3659, audit #3645 F13).**

`update_event_status` returned silently when its connection open failed and `update_event_status_with_conn` discarded the UPDATE's result; `record_dispatch*` did the same for the per-subscription counters. The persisted delivery history (`subscription_events.delivery_status`, `subscriptions.dispatch_count` / `failure_count`) could therefore contradict the wire outcome with nothing logged or counted, while `doctor`'s success rate and K7 replay read it as truth. Now every bookkeeping write is observed by the new `subscriptions::audit_status` module: an ERROR log carrying the subscription and correlation id, a per-stage counter (`open` | `status_update` | `status_no_row` | `dispatch_counter` — zero rows matched is its own stage, because a missing pending row is a hole, not an error), `/metrics` `ai_memory_webhook_audit_update_failed_total{stage}` (closed set, pre-touched) with `..._status_persisted_total` and `..._last_failure_at_seconds`, and `/health` `webhook_audit_delivery` as a #3646 signal object. `ai-memory doctor`'s Webhook section adds `audit_rows_pending` / `audit_rows_pending_stale` (rows still `pending` past the 60 s settle window) and warns when stale rows exist. The dispatcher's non-blocking contract is unchanged: a successful delivery is still reported as delivered; what changed is that "its history was persisted" is now a measured claim. Tests force the open failure (missing directory), the UPDATE failure (dropped table), the no-row case and the counter failure independently, plus the doctor warning.


Follow-up (Conductor condition on approval): `delivery_status` is documented at the field, in `memory_subscription_replay`'s docs and in the API/CLI references — `pending` means no terminal status was recorded, which includes a settled delivery whose terminal status write failed; making that write reliable is #3735 (v1.0.1).
