SECURITY.md no longer calls the federation transport unconditionally "mutually authenticated TLS (TLS 1.3)": it is TLS with a 1.2 floor and 1.3 preferred, and client-certificate mTLS pinning is opt-in on both ends (`--mtls-allowlist` on the server, `--client-cert`/`--client-key` on the sync client), as honest-limitations §4 already disclosed (#4003).
