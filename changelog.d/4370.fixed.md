Federation `/sync/push` on a PostgreSQL-backed receiver now acknowledges the loser of a concurrent delivery of the same signal id as the converged no-op, exactly as SQLite does for a duplicate. Two deliveries that both passed the existence probes raced to the `signals` insert and the loser failed on the primary key; it was counted as skipped although the row exists, so a sender that retries on skipped kept resending, and it had been charged storage quota for a row it never wrote. The loser is now classified by SQLSTATE 23505 on the `signals` primary-key constraint (any other failure still fails closed), counted as a no-op, and its quota charge is refunded exactly. The checkpoint-resolution receive had the same shape (its lost-insert classifier never matched the error the adapter actually produces) and now converges through first-resolution-wins (#4370).
