**A write of the process-global `PermissionsMode` without a live `lock_permissions_mode_for_test` guard is now a CI failure (#4491, #4763-#4767).** The discipline was convention only and recurred three times (#1465, #2165, #4468: the `store::postgres::tests::live_governance_*` cells flaked on every `--test-threads=4` governance run). `scripts/check_permissions_mode_lock.py` scans `src/**` function by function: every call of `set_active_permissions_mode`, `override_active_permissions_mode_for_test` or `clear_permissions_mode_override_for_test` is refused unless it is `main` in `src/main.rs` (the boot setter) or the `config.rs` alias, or the enclosing fn holds a LIVE guard at that call: bound by `let <ident>` in a block that encloses the write, not mentioned again before it (a rebind, drop, move or reassignment ends its cover); a write inside any closure body or `async` block is covered only by a guard bound inside that body, because deferred execution can outlive the enclosing guard (#4766); a guard binding with a `cfg`/`cfg_attr` attribute is not held (#4777). The lock is the single top-level `lock_permissions_mode_for_test` of `src/config.rs`: a definition elsewhere, a missing or duplicate one, a `use .. as` rename to or from it, an import from another module and any non-call mention are refused (#4776). The slot static `ACTIVE_PERMISSIONS_MODE` is modelled too: outside the setters it may only be read or written in one `*SLOT.write()..` statement, and a rename, re-export, import outside `config.rs`, reference, let-bound guard or raw pointer is refused (#4763). The allowlist is the unique, attribute-free (no `cfg`/`cfg_attr`), unreferenced top-level `fn main` of `src/main.rs` plus the `config.rs` setter bodies (#4764); test attributes are recognised with a leading `::`, as `r#test`, through a `use .. as` rename and through `cfg_attr`. A delegate (`rules_scope`, `pin_governance_enforce_for_test`, ...) counts only when its tail and every `return` yield the guard (or a type owning it); a `Drop` reset is accepted only for a type a delegate returns. A `#[test]` fn on a line the #3623 boundary mis-classes as production, a `cfg(any(test, ..))` item, and a test module in an out-of-line file are all checked (the boundary is a secondary `--parity` check plus a visible note, `src/bench.rs` being #4755). Writer aliasing (`use .. as`, fn pointers) and token pasting (`paste`, `concat_idents`) are refused (#4779); the lexer handles every literal prefix (`b`, `br`, `c`, `cr`, `b'`, #4778). The scan set is the compiled set: `mod` declarations (including `#[path]`), `include!` and `.rs` `include_str!` are followed, so `tests/unit/archive_gc_3383.rs` is now scanned (593 to 594 files); an unreadable or non-UTF-8 file, an unreadable or symlinked directory, and an unresolvable `mod` or `include!` exit 2 (#4774, #4775). Its known fail-closed false positives are listed in the script's docstring. It has a 141-case `--self-test` and a `--mutation-sweep` proving each of its 46 checks is load-bearing, runs as the `permissions-mode-lock-gate` job in `c8-precheck.yml`, and is red on the four `live_governance_*` cells with the #4468 fix reverted. `tests/` is tracked separately as #4754.
