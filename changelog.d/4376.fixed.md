**Escalation refusals name the settled outcome on every owning write site (#4376).** The #4116 rewrite of a governance-escalated refusal to its real outcome (`pending_id=<id>` exists, or not queued) covered the four named storage funnels and the owning `in_write_txn`. Five further sites own a `WriteTxn` and reach the pre-write consult inside it but ended with a plain rollback, so their callers read the caller-owned "deferred" wording after the transaction had already ended: `SqliteStore::store_batch`, `SqliteStore::store_with_embedding_no_overwrite`, `SqliteStore::update`, `reflect_with_hooks_for_caller` and the synthesis merge bail-outs. Each now ends its error path with `WriteTxn::rollback_resolving`, so the SAL/HTTP batch store, reflect, synthesis and SAL update report the definitive answer instead of asking the operator to poll `memory_pending_approve`.
