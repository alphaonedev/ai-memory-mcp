**Ctrl-C and SIGTERM handling (#8193, #6236, #6237, #6385, #6869).** One shared
stop primitive (`stop_signal`) now serves `serve`, `wake-hub`, `wake-listen`,
`watch`, `curator` and `sync-daemon`. `serve` drains, writes its final witness
and exits 0 on SIGTERM exactly as on SIGINT, `sync-daemon` no longer dies on
SIGTERM, and the Dockerfile sets `STOPSIGNAL SIGTERM`. A signal registration
that fails is logged as an error and never read as a received stop (that
signal then ends the process undrained, as with no handler). The primitive is
installed once, before the daemon serves; `wake-listen` no longer drops a stop
signal that arrives during `--exec`. The guide states the 90 s container stop
budget.
