**The env-mutation gate resolves the IP-host placeholder by scope (#8035).** `{host}` in a reqwest URL was resolved through any `let host` in the test fn or its callees, so a same-named helper parameter, `for` variable, closure parameter or pattern binding shadowed the check. Each URL is now judged inside its own fn against a plain `let` that precedes it; any other binding of the name keeps the reader. Test-infrastructure only.
