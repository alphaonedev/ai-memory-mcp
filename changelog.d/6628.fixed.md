**The sync daemon no longer persists a peer credential as its `sync_state` key (#6628, #6700, CWE-312).** The durable peer key was decided by a fixed list of shapes plus a raw-text `://` scan, so a numeric-prefixed password (parsed as the port), a password-less token holding `/`, `?` or `#`, and a scheme with no slashes and `://` inside the secret were keyed by credential bytes. The key check now uses only the url-crate PARSED value: a peer URL with a literal `@` left after its authority is refused before any `sync_state` read, write or #3675 rekey, the refusal names only the redacted key and says to percent-encode a literal `@` as `%40`, and a row an older daemon keyed by that raw URL is deleted (logged by the redacted key). Well-formed `user:password@host` peers and `%40` paths keep working. Regression: `url_display::tests::*_6628`, `storage::sync_state_rekey::tests::*_6628`, `tests/sync_state_peer_at_after_authority_6628.rs`.
