CI disk (#8216): new `scripts/ci/runner_disk_hook.py` (started through the one-line `scripts/ci/runner_disk_hook.sh`) and `scripts/ci/safe_remove.py`, the GitHub Actions job-started and job-completed hook for the self-hosted runners. Below 150 GiB free after a job (60 GiB at job start) it removes the cargo `target` directories of its own runner's work folder, and at job start it also removes them when the work folder is over a named allocated-bytes ceiling (`WORK_FOLDER_CEILING_GIB`, 40 GiB; the ceiling is checked at job start and end and does not bound growth inside one job). A directory is removed only at `<work root>/<repo>/target`, opened once by file descriptor with `O_NOFOLLOW` whose `CACHEDIR.TAG` carries the cargo signature, on one device, with the tag removed last; a failed free-space read refuses to decide. At job start, free space still under the floor after the clear exits 75 (`ENFORCE_START_FLOOR`; `job-completed` always exits 0) with one fixed `decision=below-floor` line. Every stderr and log text goes through one escaping funnel, and the log is created `0600` (directory `0700`). Operator notes, wiring and rollout rule: `docs/ci/runner-disk-hook.md`.
