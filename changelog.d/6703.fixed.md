**Legacy raw-URL `sync_state` keys are scrubbed at sync-daemon boot and counted by `doctor` (#6703, CWE-312, residual of #6101).** Before #3675 the sync daemon keyed `sync_state` by the raw peer URL, credential included; the #3675 heal only reaches a peer the daemon still cycles, and #6101 / #6628 refuse a peer without a durable key before any heal, so the credential stayed at rest (and in every backup) for a peer that is no longer configured or is refused. `ai-memory sync-daemon` now scrubs every such row once at boot: a durable key is folded into its rendered key with its cursors, any other row is deleted (its cursor resets; pulls are idempotent upserts, so nothing is lost). `doctor` reports the count as `legacy_raw_peer_keys` (Warning when > 0) and never the key. Deleting the cursor follows 3-agent vote (6def5ab6), option A. Regression: `tests/sync_state_legacy_raw_key_scrub_6703.rs`, `sync_state_rekey::tests::scrub_folds_durable_and_deletes_refused_raw_keys_6703`.
