**The SAL consolidation sweep halts on a rollback write-ahead failure (#4821).**
When the write-ahead row for a cluster could not be stored, `ConsolidationPass`
skipped that cluster but carried on with the rest of the sweep: it kept calling
the LLM to summarise later clusters and kept retrying write-aheads against a
rollback log that had just failed. No source was ever consumed without its
write-ahead row, but the cycle spent LLM budget for nothing and learned of the
degradation only after the pass returned. The sweep now stops at the first
write-ahead failure, the same fail-closed shape as the #3116 autonomy halt: the
remaining clusters are deferred untouched, the report carries
`halted_on_rollback_degraded`, and the curator folds the failure into the
cycle's `rollback_log_degraded` flag (#3170). Refs #6055.
