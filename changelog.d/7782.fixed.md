- **CI and coverage no longer re-run and cancel themselves on PR title and body edits** ([#7782](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/7782)). `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and `.github/workflows/coverage.yml` now subscribe to `pull_request` types `opened, synchronize, reopened` only; the `edited` type added for #7072 cancelled the in-flight run of an unchanged head on every body edit. A base retarget is re-run by closing and reopening the PR.
