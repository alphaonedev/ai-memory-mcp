### Fixed

- **`curator --once` no longer hangs with tracing enabled**
  ([#8779](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/8779)). The CLI dispatch arms
  took the process stdout/stderr locks and held them across the `spawn_blocking` sweep, so the
  first tracing event on the worker thread blocked on the stderr lock and the command never
  returned. Every dispatch arm (`src/daemon_runtime.rs` and the `inbox`, `reown`, `wake-hub`,
  `wake-listen` and `watch` verbs) now writes through the unlocked handles, which lock per
  write. Guards: `tests/stdio_lock_never_held_in_dispatch_8779.rs` (a ratchet over every
  `stdout().lock()` / `stderr().lock()` site) and `tests/curator_once_bounded_8779.rs` (the real
  binary under `RUST_LOG=trace` against a loopback stub LLM, killed at a 90 s ceiling).
