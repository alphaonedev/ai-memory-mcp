### Security

- **[redaction] The `sqlite://` store-URL branch is case-insensitive and fail-closed**
  ([#6107](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6107)). `sqlite://svc:<pw>@x/db` was
  printed unchanged while `SQLITE://` was redacted; every case of the scheme now renders through one
  branch, and a value with `@` or `=` after the scheme renders as `sqlite://<redacted-authority>`.
