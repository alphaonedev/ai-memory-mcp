**CI: self-heal the AGE extension on the macos-fed runner (#6161).** A Homebrew
upgrade relinks the `postgresql@18` trees and drops the hand-built Apache AGE
files, failing `Configure enterprise-fed tier` with `extension "age" is not
available`. New `scripts/ci/ensure-age-extension.py` probes `pg_available_extensions`
and, when `age` is missing, restores the control/SQL files and `age.dylib` from a
node-local brew-independent directory before `CREATE EXTENSION` (macos-fed only;
no-op when present; fails closed with one message otherwise). Pinned by
`scripts/test/test_ensure_age_extension_6161.py`.
