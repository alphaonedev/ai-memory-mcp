Self-hosted macOS CI legs no longer fail in `actions/checkout` with `EPERM` on a leftover append-only `.local-runs/.tmp*/audit/audit.log` (#5657). A pre-checkout step (`scripts/clear-append-only-scratch.py`, pinned byte-identical to its inline workflow copies) clears the append-only bit on the dead scratch tree only; the live audit-log flag in `src/audit.rs` is unchanged. The janitor descends by directory descriptor with `O_NOFOLLOW|O_DIRECTORY` and verifies each reopen against the scanned inode, so it cannot be redirected out of the scratch tree by a symlink or a directory swapped underneath it; it widens and restores a permission-blocked mode it owns rather than reporting it, so a leftover that `actions/checkout` would have cleaned by itself never reds a leg; and it fails closed on anything it could not prove unlinkable - a surviving `uchg`/immutable flag, a flag state it could not read, a multiply-linked flagged inode, a flagged symlink, or an inode owned by another user.
