Self-hosted macOS CI legs no longer fail in `actions/checkout` with `EPERM` on a leftover append-only `.local-runs/.tmp*/audit/audit.log` (#5657). A pre-checkout step (`scripts/clear-append-only-scratch.py`, pinned byte-identical to its inline workflow copies) clears the append-only bit on the dead scratch tree only; the live audit-log flag in `src/audit.rs` is unchanged. The janitor descends by directory descriptor with `O_NOFOLLOW|O_DIRECTORY` and verifies every open against the scanned inode - the first open of an entry and the reopen after a widen alike (#5995) - so the descent cannot be redirected out of the scratch tree by a symlink or a directory swapped underneath it. A permission-blocked mode it owns is widened and restored rather than reported, so a leftover that `actions/checkout` would have cleaned by itself never reds a leg; the widen never follows a symlink and is put back on every exit from the open, including the refusal of a swapped entry and a reopen that fails (#5812, #5813), and a restore the kernel refuses is reported with the path and both modes and reds the leg rather than being swallowed (#6002); it is held for one lookup rather than for a whole subtree descent, and every widen is first appended, as a line of its own naming the inode and both modes, to a pending-restore journal in the scratch root and fsynced before the chmod it covers, so a run killed by an uncatchable `SIGKILL` leaves lines that the next run's walk finishes on the inode it reaches - see #6006 for the journal and what it refuses. On Linux it is bound to the inode that was scanned - pinned with `O_PATH|O_NOFOLLOW`, identity-checked before anything changes, and applied through `/proc/self/fd`; on macOS, which has no `O_PATH`, it is `fchmodat(AT_SYMLINK_NOFOLLOW)` by name inside a bracket of two identity reads, which does not follow a symlink and refuses a swap that lands before the chmod, but is not bound to the scanned inode, so a swap that lands inside the bracket is reported with the mode left applied rather than undone (#5852, #5812); where neither route is available it is refused outright with nothing changed (#6005); and it fails closed on anything it could not prove unlinkable - a surviving `uchg`/immutable flag, a flag state it could not read, a multiply-linked flagged inode, a flagged symlink, an inode owned by another user, or a platform where the descriptor route it widens through is unusable, where it refuses to widen at all rather than exiting 0 over a flag it never read (#5996). The suite that pins all of this builds no fixture with privilege, removes its workspace with an owner-only `chmod -R u+rwX` (#6003), and restores every mode it narrows when a run is cancelled by a signal it can catch (`SIGINT`, `SIGTERM`, `SIGHUP`) (#5814, #6000); a suite killed by an uncatchable `SIGKILL` runs none of that, and a fixture it narrowed at that instant stays narrowed (#6007).
