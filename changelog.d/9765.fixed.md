**Docs-links guard on macOS (#9765).** `scripts/ci/tests/test_docs_relative_links_8291.py`
no longer hard-codes the Linux kernel limits: the symlink-chain rows take their
length from a probe of the host kernel (the longest chain `os.stat` follows, ended
by the first `ELOOP`), the PATH_MAX fixture and the overlong-name row take their
sizes from `os.pathconf` (`PC_NAME_MAX`, `PC_PATH_MAX`), and the `dir_fd` skip is
an assertion. A host with NAME_MAX 255 and PATH_MAX 4096 builds the fixture it
built before. The checker is unchanged.
