**Restore-fidelity matrix (#9944).** `tests/restore_fidelity_9944.rs` (SQLite: CLI
backup/restore, `export --full`/`import`, the convenience export/import, archive then
restore) and `tests/restore_fidelity_9944_pg.rs` (Postgres: admin export/import,
archive then restore) take a typed snapshot of every table and every column of the
store and compare the restored store with its source through one shared comparison
(`tests/common/restore_snapshot.rs`). Every column a path does not carry is named with
its reason, and a ratchet fails when a live table or column is left unclassified. Two
defects the matrix found stay red until their fixes land: `export --full` drops each
link's `attest_level` (#9948), and `PostgresStore::store_with_embedding` writes no
local `metadata.version_vector` (#9949).
