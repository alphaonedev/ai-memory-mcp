A judge-signed governance verdict (and the CLI stopper enforcement anchor) now names the policy version that actually evaluated it. `check_agent_action` loaded and evaluated the rules, then read the policy sequence and digest afterwards in separate autocommit statements, so a signed rule change committed by another connection or process in between produced a verdict evaluated under one policy but signed as another. The rules and the policy version are now read in one SQLite read snapshot and carried through evaluation and emission (the per-instance rule cache stores them together), and `current_policy_version` reads the sequence and digest in one snapshot, so the pair always names a single committed policy (#4044).
