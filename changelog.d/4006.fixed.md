The `serve --tls-cert` help no longer promises "plain HTTP" without the flags — `serve` uses installed or locally generated TLS material and refuses to bind otherwise — and CLI_REFERENCE / docs/SECURITY.md no longer defer `ai-memory tls init|import` to v1.0.1: v1.0.0 ships `tls init`, `tls import` and `tls status`; only `tls renew` for imported material and `db check-tls` are unshipped. Rotation is documented as re-running `tls import` (or replacing the flag files) and restarting (#4006).
