**The approver is inside the approval HMAC (#8495).** The K10 decide signature on `POST /api/v1/approvals/{pending_id}`, `POST /api/v1/pending/{id}/approve` and `POST /api/v1/pending/{id}/reject` is now signed over the versioned layout 2 subject `<pending_id>@<X-Agent-Id>` (the subject the agent api-key approval route already used), so a signature names the approver it was made for and a request that presents it under another `X-Agent-Id` is refused `401`. A layout 1 signature (subject `<pending_id>` alone) is refused `401 {"error":"legacy_unbound_approver","signature_layout_version":2}` with a hint naming the new subject, and its nonce is not consumed, so the client can re-sign. Under `identity_mode = enforce` the three decide routes also refuse a caller whose `X-Agent-Id` is not bound to its credential (`403 attested_identity_required`), so one shared secret cannot cast every vote of a consensus quorum. Signers must move to the new subject.
