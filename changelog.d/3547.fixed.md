**Fixed: evidence producers are tracked and bound to the binary they addressed (#3547).**

Harnesses that produced published figures lived only on one host under
`.local-runs/` (gitignored) or had no producer at all. `scripts/evidence/`
now holds the continuity and Big-10 producers with repo-relative paths, a
producer map, and `write-bundle.sh` which hashes the addressed executable
(never a typed digest) and stamps `run_id` + `source_commit`.
`scripts/check-evidence-bundle.sh --self-test` refuses an untracked
producer, a self-report PASS, a binary-hash mismatch, a mean-of-p99, and
a prose verdict. The unpublished MCP-tools percentage is not regenerated
without a named run capture.
