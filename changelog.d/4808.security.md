### Security

- `scripts/check-docs-no-argv-secrets.py` flags a `psql -v`/`--set`/`--variable` assignment for any secret-named variable (`db_password`, `new_pw`, `secret`, ...), behind a line continuation, in a combined short flag (`-qv`), in an abbreviated long option (`--vari=`) and when `psql` is reached through a variable. (#4808, #5284)
