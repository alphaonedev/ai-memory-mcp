The CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION docs gate now bounds each schema-claim-history ledger entry to exactly one hit (#5809): one number on one line, however many views of the line see it. An entry that shields the same span on two lines, or twice on one line, fails and names every file:line; a hit that two entries shield fails and names both entry lines; an entry that shields nothing still fails as stale. The two byte-identical history spans in ROADMAP.md and docs/v1.0.0/release-notes.md are reworded once each and given their own ledger entries.
