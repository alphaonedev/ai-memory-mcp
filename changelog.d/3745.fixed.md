- **[data-integrity] A webhook delivery could sit unstarted on the tokio
  blocking pool behind a sibling delivery's whole retry ladder**
  ([#3745](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3745),
  [#3940](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3940)).
  `Cargo.lock` pinned tokio 1.52.0. In that release, a `spawn_blocking` task
  could be handed to an idle thread without the thread being reserved for
  it. Two blocking tasks submitted while one thread was idle then shared that
  one thread, and the second waited until the first returned. Upstream calls
  this bug "causes `spawn_blocking` to hang" (tokio#8056). It was reverted in
  1.52.1 by tokio#8057.
  - **Effect.** Each webhook delivery runs on a blocking thread, and a failed
    one holds it for up to ~26 s of ACK timeouts and backoff. Under the bug,
    a second delivery, or a refused one whose DLQ write takes milliseconds,
    could start only after that ladder ended.
  - **Test effect.** The #3711 and k7 DLQ test cells went red intermittently
    for the same reason. Their poll loop was itself a long `spawn_blocking`
    task and took the only thread.
  - **Not a lock.** The futex parking in #3745's thread dump is ordinary
    idle-thread and `reqwest::blocking` waiting, not a lock in the
    dispatcher.
  - **Fix.** tokio is now locked at 1.53.1. `Cargo.toml` requires at least
    1.52.1 for both the runtime and the dev dependency, so the broken release
    cannot be resolved again.
  - **Regression test.** `tests/spawn_blocking_no_starvation_3745.rs` pins the
    pool behaviour through the tokio the crate links. The pool keeps exactly
    one thread idle, two `spawn_blocking` calls arrive back to back, and the
    first task can finish only after the second has run. On 1.52.0 the test
    fails on its first starved trial. On 1.53.1 it passes every trial.
  - **Shutdown loss of queued deliveries: fixed separately by #3979.** A
    delivery still waiting for a `DISPATCH_SEMAPHORE` permit when the 30 s
    shutdown drain expired lost both its audit row and its DLQ row, and the
    shutdown WARN claimed otherwise. The drain now records such deliveries
    to the DLQ; a crash still loses them (see
    [#3979](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3979)).
  - **Unproven: one #3745 observation.** The tokio bug explains deliveries
    that never started. It does not explain one #3745 observation: a worker
    that finished its retry ladder and then never recorded its DLQ row. That
    observation is attributed to the test harness but not proven (see #3745).
