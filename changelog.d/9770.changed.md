CI test hardening (#9770, test-only): the rust-cache pair test in `scripts/ci/tests/test_rust_cache_save_scope_9681.py` read the cache-key environment only from the workflow `env` and the job `env`, so a step-level `env:` on a `Swatinem/rust-cache` step or on a mirrored cargo step, a changed `uses`, `with`, `run` or `if`, and a changed job-level `runs-on`, `defaults`, `container`, `needs` or `permissions` went unseen between a pull-request job and its warm job in `rust-cache-warm.yml`. The test now compares, for every pair, the whole rust-cache step and each mirrored cargo step on both sides, plus every job-level key, the effective workflow and job environment and the workflow `defaults`, against a hand-written table with a closed allowed-difference list; any key that is not in the table fails unless it is equal on both sides, and an unknown key fails naming the workflow, the job, the step and the key. No workflow file, check name or other test module changes.
