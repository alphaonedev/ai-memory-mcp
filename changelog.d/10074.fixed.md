**One daemon signature, one domain, one preimage (#10074).** Every daemon
signature is now made under a domain tag, and a `signed_events` row
verifies only under the ledger-row tag, so a signature made for another
meaning no longer verifies as a ledger row. The two untagged legacy forms
verify only at or below one signed legacy cutoff row. That row binds the
cutoff sequence, the prefix chain hashes, the payload-only count and the
database identity. A missing, duplicated or invalid cutoff means no legacy
form is accepted. Above the cutoff a payload-only row is reported as
`LegacyPayloadOnlyAfterCutoff` in `signature_failures`. The cutoff is minted
once, only by `verify-signed-events-chain --mint-legacy-cutoff`. SQLite and
PostgreSQL give the same results, and stored signatures are never rewritten.
