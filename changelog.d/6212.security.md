### Security

- **[ci] The CLAUDE.md rule comparison escapes control characters of head text**
  ([#6212](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6212), follow-up of
  [#6163](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6163), CWE-150).
  `scripts/claude-md-rule-compare.py` wrote approval trailer values and
  changed rule text to the step summary and the job log with ESC, CSI, BEL
  and other control or format characters intact, so head text could send a
  terminal escape sequence or a bidirectional override to the reader. Every
  control, format, surrogate and line or paragraph separator character
  except newline and tab is now written as a `\xHH` / `\uHHHH` escape in
  diff blocks, inline spans and the fail-closed message (which is also
  masked and put on one line). Regression cells: the `#6212` self-test cells.
