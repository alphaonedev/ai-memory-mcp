### Fixed

- **[recall] `boot` / `session_start` payloads no longer spend the slot
  budget on near-duplicate stores of the same fact**
  ([#3352](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/3352)).
  `ai-memory boot --limit 10` and `memory_session_start` listed the N
  most-recently-updated rows verbatim, so four near-identical Grok-channel
  entries (different titles, minutes apart; `check-duplicate` ≥ 0.89)
  crowded out distinct context. Both surfaces now over-fetch up to the
  existing 50-row cap, cluster by cosine (when stored vectors are
  comparable, at `DUPLICATE_THRESHOLD_DEFAULT`) or lexical Jaccard of
  `title + content` (0.70, un-embedded / keyword-tier path; skipped for
  bodies under 64 characters so short fixtures cannot false-merge), keep
  the newest/highest-priority representative, and note `N similar` on the
  collapsed row. Clusters never cross namespaces. Display-only — no stored
  row is merged or deleted. Test: 5 near-duplicates + 5 distinct → 6 boot
  / session_start lines.
