**The doc symbol-anchor gate no longer reports a generic opener written with a space after it (#5530).** The comparison rule of #5498 treated every `<` followed by a space, tab or `=` as a comparison, so `Vec< src/x.rs::T<U>>` (a valid opener with unusual spacing) lost its group and the closer after the anchor was reported. A `<` is a comparison only with whitespace on both sides. Base: accepted; tip before this fix: reported; accepted after this fix, then refused as `UNDECIDABLE_LT` by #5608, because a `<` followed by whitespace is not provably an opener and the anchor is judged differently either way. Real tree: 0 lines change. The `--self-test` gains 4 cases (#5530).
