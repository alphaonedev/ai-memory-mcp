**Approved deletes and promotions on SQLite are owner-gated (#8564).** On the SQLite store, an approved `delete` pending action and an approved tier-bump `promote` executed on the target row whoever owned it. Both arms now run under the requester's owner gate, the same as the Postgres backend: a row owned by another agent, or a row with no owner stamp, is refused with `PermissionDenied` and left unchanged, and the requester's own row executes.
