**Test: raise `wait_for_health` spawn ceiling 10s → 60s and surface stderr on timeout (#3615).**

`tests/integration.rs::wait_for_health` gave a freshly spawned `ai-memory serve` 10 s (100 × 100 ms) under a full `cargo test --no-fail-fast` run; one of 27 concurrent `DaemonGuard` daemons missed that ceiling and panicked `serve never came up` with `Stdio::null()` discarding the child's stderr. The loop still returns on the first 200 (healthy spawns pay only time-to-ready). Ceiling is now 60 s, multiplied by `AI_MEMORY_TEST_TIMING_BUDGET_MULT` (env #68); a child that exits fails immediately via `try_wait`; stderr is captured under `integration_scratch_root()` and the tail is included in the panic. Test-infra only; no product bytes.
