**[release][test] The fast condition-anchor check has fixtures of its own (#6954, umbrella #6062).**
`condition_anchor_failures` (the seconds-long check that refuses a
duplicated condition-mutant anchor or a replacement holding another anchor)
ran only over the live table, where neither refusal fires, so weakening
either test left `--self-test` green. It now takes the source and table as
arguments, and the self-test pins a duplicated anchor (1 refusal), a
replacement holding another anchor (1) and a clean table (0); the two
weakenings are condition mutants of the sweep. Regression: the
`condition anchor fixture` self-test cases.
