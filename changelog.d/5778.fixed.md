**The doc symbol-anchor gate reads a raw identifier (#5778).** A qualified anchor to a raw identifier (`src/x.rs::T::r#m`) was cited as `src/x.rs::r`, a false report with the wrong name, and a raw-identifier link label was not checked at all. The identifier patterns now take an optional `r#`: the component is looked up by its name, indexed from `fn r#name` too, and cited as written; `r#crate`, `r#self`, `r#super`, `r#Self` and `r#_`, which Rust rejects, are refused as UNMODELLED. The `--self-test` gains 10 cases (#5778).
