**`ai-memory auto-consolidate` keeps every source's full text and no longer panics on non-ASCII content (#4277).**
The auto-consolidate verb built the merged row from a raw 200-byte slice of each
source. A multi-byte character spanning byte 200 panicked the verb, and in every
other case the bytes past 200 were dropped from the merged row before the sources
were consumed (hard-deleted under the legacy disposition), so the tail of every
long source was lost. The merged row now carries each source's full text. A
group whose combined text exceeds the per-memory content limit is skipped with
a stderr note and its sources are left untouched, never truncated. Refs #6055.
