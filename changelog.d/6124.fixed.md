### Fixed

- **[gate][federation] Cert-expiry rule (B) has an honest pass while the
  certification is VOID or EXPIRED**
  ([#6124](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6124)).
  With STATUS VOID or EXPIRED at both ends of a change, re-issue is not
  possible, so `scripts/check_cert_expiry.py` failed every watched-surface
  change with no way to pass. A change now passes when it appends new dated
  records to the append-only amendment ledger at the end of
  `docs/compliance/ENTERPRISE-FEDERATION-CERTIFICATION.md` naming exactly
  every changed watched path and `AI_MEMORY_FED_*` identifier. A missing or
  extra name, an out-of-window or decreasing date, an edited or removed
  earlier record, or any change of Binds-to while VOID/EXPIRED (a drop to
  `-` included) is refused with an exact reason. The ledger is a strict
  Markdown subset and anything unrecognised is a refusal. An amendment never
  discharges the certification, and LIVE behaviour is unchanged.
- Before any pass decision one clean-banner precondition runs at both ends
  of the range: exactly one strict STATUS line, at most one strict Binds-to
  line and no banner-like line the strict grammar does not read (look-alike
  letters, zero-width characters, other heading levels, nested quotes and
  `Binds-to` spellings are read by a looser detector and refused). A change
  of the STATUS word between VOID and EXPIRED is routed to the amendment
  rule, never to the re-issue pass. On an amendment pass the STATUS line is
  frozen byte for byte; only the status word may change, between VOID and
  EXPIRED. The record date ceiling is the runner's UTC date, not the
  author-set commit date.
- New or changed refusal texts (each ends with its line where it has one):
  - `banner (base|head): a second STATUS line under the strict grammar`,
    `... a second Binds-to line under the strict grammar`,
    `... a STATUS-like line the strict grammar does not read`,
    `... a Binds-like line the strict grammar does not read`,
    `... the document is N bytes, over the 8388608-byte limit`; each is
    followed by the #3556 text chosen from the parsed status (no parseable
    STATUS line, two or more STATUS banner lines, two or more Binds-to
    lines) and `The cert doc at ... does not carry exactly one STATUS
    banner line and at most one Binds-to line (parsed: ...)`.
  - `the STATUS line at <doc>:<line> is frozen while the certification is
    not LIVE: on an amendment pass it must be byte-identical to the range
    start (only the status word may change, between VOID and EXPIRED); put
    the reason for the change in the amendment ledger record`.
  - `Binds-to set|dropped|changed (A → B) while STATUS stays|goes ...: an
    amendment never rebinds; re-issue as LIVE`.
  - `The STATUS word changed X → Y; a change between VOID and EXPIRED is
    not a re-issue and not a voiding record (#6124).`
  - `record dated D is after the runner's UTC date T plus 1 day` and
    `record dated D is before the previous record dated P; record dates
    must not decrease`.
  - `item lines must be sorted and unique (<item order>; this line is not
    after the previous one)`.
  - Above the ledger: `line longer than 4096 bytes above the ledger`,
    `a second '<!--' on one line above the ledger`, `a fence body naming an
    amendment or a status above the ledger`, `a link reference definition
    naming an amendment or a status above the ledger`, `a setext heading
    naming an amendment or a status above the ledger`, `emphasis naming an
    amendment or a status above the ledger`, `inline HTML naming an
    amendment or a status above the ledger`, `an HTML comment not closed on
    its line above the ledger`.
