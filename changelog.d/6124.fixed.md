`> **Amendment (YYYY-MM-DD, ...).**` record below STATUS, plus at most one blank separator
line, changing no other line of the doc: its header alone on its line and opening its own
paragraph; placed directly above an existing amendment header or as the last paragraph of
its blockquote; outside CommonMark code fences, every HTML block kind and indented code;
dated from the day of its own commit less one (at most 14 days below the merge-base day) to today plus one; every line plain
printable text, a bullet entry or the back line; listing exactly every changed watched
path and identifier; and citing only #6063 (WP-B1) by its issue URL as the path back to
LIVE, with no other issue, commit, link or autolink anywhere in the record. The cert doc
is read from the object database, never through a symlink, under a 2 MiB cap and as
strict UTF-8; a read failure at either end fails closed. While the banner is EXPIRED or
VOID the failure headline names the non-discharging amendment (not a re-issue) and the
remedy names the record form, where it may go and its exact entries. The amendment is a
non-discharging record, never a re-mint; LIVE banners keep rule B and rule C unchanged.
