### Fixed

- **[gate][federation] Cert-expiry rule (B) has an honest pass while the
  certification is VOID or EXPIRED**
  ([#6124](https://github.com/alphaonedev/ai-memory-mcp/issues/6124)).
  With STATUS VOID or EXPIRED at both ends of a change, re-issue is not
  possible, so `scripts/check_cert_expiry.py` failed every watched-surface
  change with no way to pass. A change now passes when it appends new dated
  records to the append-only amendment ledger at the end of
  `docs/compliance/ENTERPRISE-FEDERATION-CERTIFICATION.md` naming exactly
  every changed watched path and `AI_MEMORY_FED_*` identifier. A missing or
  extra name, an out-of-window date, an edited or removed earlier record, or a
  new or changed Binds-to while VOID/EXPIRED is
  refused with an exact reason. The ledger is a strict Markdown subset and
  anything unrecognised is a refusal. An amendment never discharges the
  certification, and LIVE behaviour is unchanged.
