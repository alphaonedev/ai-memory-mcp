**The release-feature guard compares every pinned action to its USES constant (#8030).**
Where a USES constant exists (checkout, buildx, login, image build, attest), every
`uses:` of that action in release.yml, release-shape.yml, c8-precheck.yml and
publish-ci-image.yml must equal it; a re-pin to another 40-hex sha is refused with
the workflow, job, step and constant named. Tests: `PerAction8030`.
Round 5 (security review, umbrella #8199): the comparison is made on the case-folded
owner/repo with any subpath kept, an empty, `.` or `..` path segment is refused, every
job container and service image must be pinned by digest, a job needs a non-empty list
of mapping steps, a `run:` may not download and execute or splice a `github.event`,
`github.head_ref`, `github.ref_name` or `inputs` expression into the script, and a local
`./` action is loaded from the tree and held to the same rules. Tests: `Round5`.
