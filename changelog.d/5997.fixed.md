The append-only scratch janitor's widen paths are pinned by behaviour on both platforms (#5997, #5998, #5999, #6004, #6005, #6017, #6021, #6022, #5936). On Linux an entry replaced before its inode is pinned is refused with nothing widened (#5997), and a pin that fails closes its descriptor (#6004); a reopen after a widen that lands on a replacement is refused and the replacement is left untouched (#5998); an interrupt or a failure during the widen puts the found mode back and still propagates, and a put-back the kernel refuses is reported with both modes and the path (#5999); an entry renamed away before the reopen is put back on Linux and reported as left applied on macOS (#6017); a chmod through `/proc/self/fd` that fails with `ENOENT` is reported, never read as an entry that went away (#6021); a platform with neither an inode-bound nor a no-follow chmod refuses with nothing changed (#6005); a file whose inode has a second link is never widened, including a link made after the scan classified the file, which the widen step's own re-read of the inode refuses on both platforms (#5936); and on macOS a mode hidden behind an append-only or immutable flag, which the kernel will not let a chmod change, is reported with the `chflags nouappnd` remedy instead of a bare `EPERM` (#6022).
