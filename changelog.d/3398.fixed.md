Bind `POST /api/v1/agents` to the authenticated caller: a caller may register or
refresh only its own roster entry, and managing another agent's entry now
requires the admin role. The route previously had neither an admin gate nor a
caller check while `db::register_agent` upserts the row, so any authenticated
caller could overwrite another agent's `agent_type` and `capabilities` — and the
admin-gated `GET /api/v1/agents` then served the forged entry. Refusals are
audited. The SQLite and PostgreSQL arms of the route also now share one response
projection, so a PostgreSQL-backed registration acknowledges with the same
`registered` field SQLite has always emitted.
