The PERF-3 test `dispatch_to_50_subscribers_caps_inflight_at_semaphore_bound` no longer fails on a saturated host that is slow but still delivering (#6794). A delivery that fails an attempt keeps its dispatch permit through the whole retry ladder (about 26 s by design), so a fixed drain deadline cannot tell a slow host from a leaked permit. The test now polls the drain and, if permits are still held at the deadline, fails only when the receiver served no request in the final 11 s (a leak has no worker left to send); the drain timeline is printed either way. No product code changed: the permit is moved into the `spawn_blocking` closure and released on return, panic unwind or closure drop.
