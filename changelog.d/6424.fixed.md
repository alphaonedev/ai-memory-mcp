**The link-tail parser of the compliance script-name gate is deleted (#6424).**
`scripts/check_compliance_script_names.py` removed link destinations and titles with a parser that diverged from
GitHub on destination escapes, control bytes, vertical tab, form feed and line endings, so text the renderer
shows could be hidden from the scan. The gate no longer parses links (#6141, design B): stale names are counted in
the raw bytes, a name fragment next to `(`, `)`, `[`, `]` or a line break is a violation, and control characters
other than tab fail the gate.
