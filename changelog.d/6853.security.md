**PR comparison masking, round 6 (#6853).** The cost of masking in `scripts/claude-md-rule-compare.py` is bounded in total, not only per line: each Redactor has a work budget of 262144 units (2 per scanned character). A diff block or text whose scan would exceed it is hidden whole before any pattern runs, and the summary says that the masking budget was exhausted. A head of the largest accepted size (2 MiB) of lines just under the 2000-character cap used to take about 248 seconds; it now takes under a second and is hidden whole.
