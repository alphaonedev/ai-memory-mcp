**Cert-expiry self-test pins log_safe's backslash doubling (#6921).**
`log_safe()` of `scripts/check_cert_expiry.py` prints a backslash as two, which
is what keeps its escape form one-to-one: a name holding a real control or
format character and a name holding the text of its escape print differently.
No cell pinned it, so a change that printed a backslash unchanged passed the
whole self-test. Cells `log-safe-backslash` and `log-safe-roundtrip` now pin the
property (a corpus of 825 names decodes back exactly). No production change.
Refs #6140, #6175, #6683, #6921.
