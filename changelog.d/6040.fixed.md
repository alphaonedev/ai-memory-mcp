A janitor run that blocks instead of refusing is now a failure rather than a wait (#6040). The suite ran the janitor with no timeout, so an ordering that waited on the journal's lock produced no result line at all instead of a red test, and the clear step in `ci.yml` and `session-boot-lifetime.yml` carried no `timeout-minutes`, leaving a blocked run to hold a self-hosted runner for GitHub's 360-minute default. Every subprocess the suite runs is now bounded and its expiry is reported with a named reason, the in-process seams are bounded by a watchdog, both workflow steps carry `timeout-minutes: 10`, and a test refuses any janitor step in CI that has no bound.
