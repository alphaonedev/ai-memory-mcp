**The cert-expiry context check sees never-taken conditions in other literal forms (#6707).** `if (false)`, `if !!false`, `if !cfg!(..)`, a `for` over an empty literal range and a match arm whose literal never equals a literal scrutinee all disabled the guarded line without a finding. Headers are normalised (parentheses dropped, double negation cancelled) before the rules run, and the range and match-arm rules are new: `(wrap-if-paren-false)`, `(wrap-if-not-not-false)`, `(wrap-if-not-cfg)`, `(wrap-for-empty-range)`, `(wrap-match-never-arm)`.
