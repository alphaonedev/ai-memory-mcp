Stop `recall`, `search`, `list` and `boot`/`session_start` from returning
substrate bookkeeping as ordinary memories. On a store shared by several agents
an unscoped recall returned other agents' `_messages/<them>` inbox mail — their
A2A traffic — ranked above the operator's own memories, and `--as-agent`
additionally returned `_agents` registry rows. Each read funnel had
reimplemented the same `caller == None` means "trust every row" shape, so the
posture was decided in five places and defaulted open in all of them. All five
now route through one predicate in the visibility SSOT,
`visibility::is_readable_on_query`: a substrate namespace (`_messages/*`,
`_inbox/*`, `_curator/*`, `_subscriptions/*`, `_standard:*`, `_agents`,
`_agent_sessions`, `_standards`) is withheld from an UNSCOPED read whatever its
scope, naming the namespace is the opt-in, and the canonical owner/inbox gate still
applies on top of it, so a caller with a resolved identity who names another
agent's inbox gets nothing. (Where no identity is configured at all, the
pre-existing single-tenant trust-all posture is deliberately unchanged — #3348
closes the AMBIENT disclosure, the one that fired on a bare `recall` and needed
no knowledge of the victim.)
The HTTP list/search and GET/POST recall funnels apply the same predicate after
either SAL/PostgreSQL or direct-SQLite retrieval, preserving backend parity.
`ai-memory boot` had no visibility filter at all and now has one. Ordinary
namespaces keep the previous behaviour byte-for-byte, so single-tenant
deployments are unaffected.
