**The webhook admission batch and the shutdown DLQ hand-off take the SQLite write lock at BEGIN (#6568).** The #3980 admission batch opened a bare `SAVEPOINT` (a DEFERRED transaction on a connection in autocommit) and the #3979 drain transfer opened a DEFERRED `transaction()`, so either could lose the write lock to a concurrent writer after it had begun. Both now open `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` through `WriteTxn::begin`; a dispatching caller already inside a transaction is joined (its own `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` holds the lock and its commit decides the batch), and `scripts/qc-codegraph-precheck.sh` (C8, #5084) is clean again.
