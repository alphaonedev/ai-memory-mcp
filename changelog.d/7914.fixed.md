**CI: bounded admission for the linux-fed enterprise-fed leg (#7914).** The self-hosted `Check (linux-fed,enterprise-fed)` leg now runs its compile and test phase under `scripts/ci/host_semaphore.py`, a host-local file-lock semaphore with 2 slots, so at most two such legs build and test at once on a shared host and the single-shot #6795 load gate is no longer the only guard; the leg cap rises from 160 to 240 minutes (hosted and macOS caps unchanged) because the semaphore wait counts against it and the serial shard needs about 7800 s after a cold compile.
