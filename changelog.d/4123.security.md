**Hugging Face Hub weight downloads follow the inference-egress posture
(#4123; GOD ruling, 5-agent vote 4d3ea1c5, 5/5 option A).** The in-process
MiniLM embedder and the cross-encoder reranker fetched their weights through
`hf_hub::api::sync::Api::new()` with no admission at all: under
`AI_MEMORY_INFERENCE_EGRESS=deny` — documented as "no inference egress at all"
— or `loopback-only` / `internal-only`, a cache miss still reached
huggingface.co at construction. Every restricted posture now REFUSES the
fetch: both models run cache-only, exactly as under `AI_MEMORY_EMBED_OFFLINE`
(the one offline guard, `Embedder::remote_fetch_disabled_reason`, now also consults
the posture). With no pre-staged cache the embedder fails closed to keyword
recall and the reranker to lexical (#1593), each with a WARN that names the
posture knob and the cache-staging remedy. `allow`, the default, is
byte-identical. Neither loader builds the Hub client through `Api::new()`
any more: the embedder builds through `ApiBuilder::from_env()`, so its ONLINE
fetch now honours `HF_HOME` (it wrote under `$HOME` while its offline resolver
read `HF_HOME`, #3788) and `HF_ENDPOINT` (a mirror); the reranker roots its
online cache where its own offline resolver reads (`$HOME`) and honours
`HF_ENDPOINT`. Pinned by
`tests/hf_hub_fetch_egress_posture_4123.rs` (RED on the pre-fix tree: under
`deny` the loaders fetched from the Hub and the refusal named no posture)
with an `allow` control and a staged-cache-still-loads cell.

Upgrade note: a `loopback-only` / `deny` / `internal-only` node (the
`asi-hard` template sets `loopback-only`) whose MiniLM or cross-encoder
weights are NOT pre-staged no longer downloads them on upgrade; semantic
recall degrades to keyword and neural reranking to lexical until the weights
are staged (`HF_HOME/hub/models--…/snapshots/…`, or
`~/.cache/huggingface/hub`; the WARN names the path). Stage them once with
`AI_MEMORY_INFERENCE_EGRESS=allow` on a connected host, or copy a populated
cache, then restart.
