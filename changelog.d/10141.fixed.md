### Fixed (#10141 — atomic transaction-bound row-attestation arming & in-tx lineage head check)

- **`identity succeed`, guardian recovery rebind, and lineage append paths now execute precondition check, attestation arming, and append inside a single atomic write transaction (`TxnArmed`).** On SQLite, standalone `arm_with_key` and `arm_for_recovery_in_tx` are replaced by the transaction-bound RAII guard `TxnArmed<'c>` (consumed-state pattern, no boolean flag), which can only be obtained from an open `WriteTxn`, advances the anchored seal only after `COMMIT` succeeds, and rolls back the transaction plus removes temporary `ra10040_*` triggers / `ATTEST_FN` (reconciling back to `Session`/`Refusing` on already-armed stores) on any early return, refusal, error, or panic unwind. `append_lineage_record`, `append_succession`, `append_revocation`, `enroll_lineage`, `register_recovery_key`, and PostgreSQL `PostgresStore::append_lineage_record` (under `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) now perform their lineage-head consistency checks inside the write transaction so concurrent racers receive exact not-head / chain-mismatch refusals instead of duplicate-epoch primary-key errors and leave no connection armed.
