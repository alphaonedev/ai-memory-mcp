**Compatibility characters that fold into a script name are reported as look-alikes (#6418).**
`scripts/check_compliance_script_names.py` matches a loose script-name pattern over the unescaped line, and any
match holding a non-ASCII character is a look-alike violation, reported with the document's own characters. A
name that appears only after NFKC folding, dash mapping or removal of combining marks and invisible characters is
reported as hidden (#6141, design B).
