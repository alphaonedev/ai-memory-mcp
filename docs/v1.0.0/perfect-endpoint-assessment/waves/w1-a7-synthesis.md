## CANONICAL DEFINITION (perfect endpoint AI memory, 1 paragraph)

**Perfect endpoint AI memory** is an **endpoint-resident, offline-capable cognitive-governance substrate** that holds *attested cognitive artifacts* (observations, decisions, refusals, reflections, plans, tombstones, lineage) — never bare world-propositions or untyped text — at every contact locus where AI/AGI/ASI meets physical, biological, or other-AI reality (Tier ∅ via gateway-held L1 when silicon cannot host the binary). It keeps identity and accumulation **coherent** across sessions and model replacement under volatile-context / frozen-weights / plural-instance facts; makes writes and the reasoning record **stoppable without silent corruption** (typed refusal as first-class state, not world-action veto); **compounds cognition outside weights** via observe→atom→reflect→skill compositions that survive model death; **attests operations** with cryptographic non-repudiation so weaker observers verify stronger cognitions without trusting them; **bias-displaces** self-accounts through architectural separation-of-powers (N≥3 *attested* model-family quorum before promotion into durable self — substrate verifies, does not trust or optimize panels); and stays **LLM-agnostic** at every cognitive boundary so multi-vendor endpoints remain governable. Indexes (FTS/HNSW), inference, and evolutionary search are access/maintenance machinery or siblings — never the ontology. It is integrity substructure for verification and decorrelation, not a knowledge base, orchestrator, cloud SaaS, or ASI control plane.

**Honesty template (A2 — binding for public/procurement):**  
> *ai-memory is endpoint-resident integrity substructure for operation attestation, record-level stoppability, cross-session artifact continuity (under frozen-weights assumptions), and (when attested) collective write decorrelation — necessary-but-not-sufficient for DeepMind AGI→ASI preparedness frictions; not a universal ASI governor, not capability attestation, not pathway-complete.*

## PERFECT SYSTEM AXIOMS (merged top 10)

Merged from A1 (11), A2 (14), A3 (10 invariants), A4 constitution, A5 physics, A6 epistemics — chair top 10:

1. **Contact-surface / Locus.** Governance that is not at the contact point (or its honest gateway) is opinion about the endpoint, not governance of it. Offline-first L1 is non-negotiable for stop/attest/cohere when radio or law is adversarial (A1, A5).
2. **Substrate ≠ cognition.** Substrate holds engagement artifacts and gates; it does not think content true, grade ASI utility, or host evolutionary self-optimization (A1, A4, A2 #12).
3. **Artifact-not-proposition / Memory ≠ Knowledge.** Bare world-facts are siblings; endpoint memory holds *who engaged what, with what kind, provenance, confidence basis, refusal, and lineage* (A1, A6, ROADMAP §4).
4. **Refusal-is-content.** Typed deny/veto/tombstone with reconstructible chain; silent drop is corruption (A1, A3).
5. **Non-repudiation of operations; content not self-authoritative.** Weaker observers verify writes without trusting producers; self-report is not evidence (A1, A2, A3).
6. **Decorrelated-self / Federalist 51.** No unilateral self enters durable bias-displaced identity without N≥3 attestably distinct foreign priors; ambition counteracts ambition (A1, A4).
7. **Generation-survival + index≠ontology.** Identity and accumulation externalize across weight/session death; vectors/FTS/HNSW are access methods, never identity (A1 #9, A6, A3 vector-death resilience).
8. **Authority-lane ≠ data-lane.** Completing actions / leases / transitions is not the same trust model as relaying observations (A1 #10).
9. **Scope & pathway honesty.** In-substrate iff strengthens ≥1 of 2.1–2.7; pathway-agnostic core is 2.3/2.5/2.6; 2.2/2.4 conditional; voluntary ledger ≠ universal governor (A2, A3, ROADMAP §3).
10. **Policy ≠ architecture; succession over immortality.** Properties held only by operator discipline are not held at ASI scale; infinity = multi-impl, crypto-agile, witness-anchored *protocol* succession — not this binary forever (A2, A3, chair).
