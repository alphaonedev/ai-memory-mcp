## Why two battery counts are lower than at `eba96b307`

`posture_control15_pg_resolution_3106` reports 3 (was 4) and
`--lib enterprise_federation_posture` 37 (was 39) under default features.
Both drops come from #4434 (`7dc81b48a`, `e7ddb40ea`, in this batch): a build
without `sal-postgres` cannot prove the postgres driver's `sslmode` pin, so
pg control #15 must FAIL there, and the cells that assert the pg PASS (legs 1
and 4 of the `_3106` binary, two lib cells) are now gated on `sal-postgres`;
a default build runs the fail-closed twin
`pg_control_15_never_passes_without_sal_postgres_4434` instead. No cell was
lost: the `--features sal-postgres` leg runs the gated cells
(`pg_dsn_resolves_control_15_to_the_compensating_control`,
`certified_pg_config_reaches_all_pass`, and the two lib cells), 4 and 39,
matching the `eba96b307` counts.

provenance line that records what actually ran is worth more than one tidied
to match the banner.
