**Recommended cut to keep 29 Sep honest.** Separate *tag-blocking* (a defect that would ship) from *certificate-blocking* (evidence about the shipped binary). §7 of the standard already provides the vocabulary: anything short of G1–G8 "is a pilot, and the vendor must say so".
