## 5. Decisive capabilities and their limits

### Encryption in transit — K09: AI-MEMORY-STRONGER, 3/3

ai-memory resolves listener certificates and refuses plaintext fallback if local TLS setup fails. Local CA bootstrap is for a declared singleton; other deployment shapes require appropriate operator material. ### Governance and fail-closed behavior — K12: PARITY, 3/3

ai-memory implements a broader general policy vocabulary: rules, hooks, Allow/Deny/Ask, signed-rule admission and approval events.
